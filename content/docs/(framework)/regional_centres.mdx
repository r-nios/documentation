# Regional Centres

NIOS Regional Centres (RCs) provide learner support, coordinate study centres, assist with examinations, admissions, and various learner services.

Select your Regional Centre below to access official contact information and regional resources.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you're unsure which Regional Centre serves your area, use the official
  Study Centre Locator or refer to your admission records. Regional Centre
  coverage may change over time.
</Callout>
