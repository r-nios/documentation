# Study Centres

NIOS Study Centres, officially known as **Accredited Institutions (AIs)**, provide academic and administrative support to learners.

They act as local support centres where students can receive guidance, attend practical classes, participate in PCP sessions, and complete various learner-related activities.

## What Does a Study Centre Do?

| Service                  | Available |
| ------------------------ | --------- |
| PCP Classes              | ✓         |
| Practical Sessions       | ✓         |
| Assignment Support       | ✓         |
| Academic Guidance        | ✓         |
| Learner Assistance       | ✓         |
| Examination Coordination | ✓         |

## When Will You Need Your Study Centre?

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Practical Examinations">
  Many practical examinations are coordinated through your study centre.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="PCP Classes">
  Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs) may be conducted through study centres.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Academic Support">
  Learners can receive guidance regarding subjects, assignments, and
  examinations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Assignment-Related Activities">
  Certain learner activities may involve interaction with the study centre.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="General Assistance">
  Study centres often act as the first point of contact for local learner
  support.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Common Questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Do I need to visit my study centre regularly?">
  Not necessarily. The frequency of visits depends on your programme, subjects,
  practical requirements, and academic activities.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my study centre?">
  In certain situations, learners may be permitted to request a study centre
  change through the appropriate procedures.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will my practical exams happen at my study centre?">
  Many practical activities are coordinated through study centres, but
  arrangements can vary depending on the programme and subject.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I know which study centre I am assigned to?">
  Your allotted study centre information is usually available through your
  admission records and learner dashboard.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

<Callout type="idea">
  Your study centre is one of the most important support resources available
  during your NIOS journey. Keep its contact information readily accessible.
</Callout>
