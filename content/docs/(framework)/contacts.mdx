## Which Contact Should You Use?

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Admission Problems">
  Use admission support channels for application issues, document verification,
  corrections, eligibility queries, and admission status updates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Hall Ticket or Examination Issues">
  Contact your Regional Centre first. For theory, practical, FA, ODE, and
  examination-related issues, examination support channels may also be helpful.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Results, Rechecking, or Re-Evaluation">
  Use the result correction services, rechecking portals, or re-evaluation
  services as applicable. Keep your enrollment number and examination details
  ready.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Certificate or Marksheet Issues">
  Use certificate verification, migration certificate, duplicate marksheet,
  duplicate certificate, and document correction services.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Study Centre Problems">
  Contact your study centre first. If the issue remains unresolved, escalate it
  to your Regional Centre.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Technical Portal Issues">
  For website errors, payment issues, login problems, and dashboard-related
  difficulties, use the grievance portal or learner support channels.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Before You Contact Support

- Check the latest notifications.
- Verify whether the issue can be resolved through e-Services.
- Keep your enrollment details ready.
- Note down application, reference, or payment numbers.
- Take screenshots of portal errors when applicable.
- Keep relevant documents ready for verification.

<Callout type="warning">
  Never share passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, or sensitive personal
  information through unofficial channels, social media accounts, or third-party
  websites.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Contact details, office timings, and support channels may change over time.
  Always verify the latest information through the official NIOS website before
  visiting an office or submitting important documents.
</Callout>
