## What is NIOS?

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) is a government-recognized education board that offers flexible learning opportunities for students across India.

Unlike traditional schools, NIOS allows learners to study at their own pace and provides greater flexibility in admissions, subject choices, and examinations.

## Why This Guide Exists

Official prospectuses and notifications often contain important information, but they can be difficult to understand without context.

This documentation aims to bridge that gap by:

- Explaining rules in plain language
- Providing practical examples
- Highlighting common mistakes
- Clarifying confusing policies
- Helping learners make informed decisions

The goal is not to promote NIOS, but to help students understand how it works in practice.
