Before applying for admission, every learner must ensure they meet the basic eligibility requirements for their chosen NIOS programme.

Eligibility is generally determined by three key factors:

- Age requirements
- Educational qualifications
- Special admission provisions (where applicable)

## How Eligibility Works

<Steps>
  <Step>

  </Step>

  <Step>

Check whether you satisfy the minimum age requirement for that programme.

  </Step>

  <Step>

Verify that you meet the required educational qualification, if applicable.

  </Step>

  <Step>

Review any special provisions that may apply to your situation.

  </Step>

  <Step>

Proceed with admission only after confirming all eligibility requirements.

  </Step>

## Why Eligibility Matters

Eligibility requirements help ensure that learners enter programmes appropriate for their academic level and circumstances.

Meeting these requirements before applying can help avoid:

- Admission delays
- Document verification issues
- Application rejection
- Unnecessary fees and corrections

<Callout type="info">
  Most learners only need to satisfy the age and educational qualification
  requirements. Special eligibility provisions apply only in specific
  situations.
</Callout>
