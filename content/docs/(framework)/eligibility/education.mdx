Apart from age requirements, NIOS also has educational eligibility criteria for different programmes.

The required qualification depends on whether you are applying for:

- Secondary (Class 10)
- Senior Secondary (Class 12)

---

## Quick Overview

| Programme                   | Required Qualification                            |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Secondary (Class 10)        | No formal previous qualification required         |
| Senior Secondary (Class 12) | Must have passed Secondary/Class 10 or equivalent |

---

## Programme Eligibility

<Tabs items={["Secondary", "Senior Secondary"]}>

<Tab value="Secondary">

### Secondary Course (Class 10)

NIOS does not require learners to pass a previous board examination for admission into the Secondary programme.

However, learners should have sufficient basic education to successfully study Class 10-level subjects.

This pathway is commonly used by:

- Students returning after a break
- Learners without formal schooling
- Adults continuing education later in life

</Tab>

<Tab value="Senior Secondary">

### Senior Secondary Course (Class 12)

To enroll in the Senior Secondary programme, learners must have completed:

- Class 10
- Secondary education
- Or an equivalent qualification from a recognized board

The qualification is required because the programme is academically equivalent to Class 12.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Common Situations

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I never completed regular schooling. Can I join NIOS Secondary?">
  Yes. Many learners use NIOS Secondary as an alternative pathway to complete
  Class 10 education.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I apply for Senior Secondary without passing Class 10?">
  No. Senior Secondary admission requires a valid Secondary/Class 10
  qualification or equivalent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I passed Class 10 from another board. Is it accepted?">
  In most cases, yes, provided the board is recognized.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can adults apply even after many years?">
  Yes. NIOS has no upper age limit, and many adult learners continue their
  education through NIOS.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Important Note About Documents

Learners applying for Senior Secondary admission are usually required to provide proof of their previous qualification during the admission process.

This may include:

- Marksheets
- Certificates
- Transfer-related documents
- Other supporting records depending on the case

<Callout type="warning">
  Submitting incorrect or invalid educational documents can lead to admission
  rejection or cancellation.
</Callout>

---

## Why These Requirements Exist

Educational qualification rules help ensure that learners enter programmes suited to their academic level.

For example:

- Secondary courses are designed for Class 10-level learners
- Senior Secondary courses assume prior Class 10 knowledge

These requirements help maintain academic continuity between programmes.
