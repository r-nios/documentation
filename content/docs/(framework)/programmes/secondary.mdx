# Secondary Programme (Class 10)

The NIOS Secondary Programme is equivalent to **Class 10** and is designed for learners who wish to complete their secondary education through a flexible learning system.

Whether you're continuing your studies, returning after a break, or looking for an alternative to regular schooling, the Secondary programme provides a recognized pathway to complete Class 10.

## Quick Overview

| Feature       | Details                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Equivalent To | Class 10                                    |
| Minimum Age   | 14 Years                                    |
| Maximum Age   | No Upper Limit                              |
| Mode of Study | Flexible & Self-Paced                       |
| Certification | Secondary Certificate                       |
| Conducted By  | National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) |

## After Completing Secondary

Successfully completing the Secondary Programme allows learners to:

- Apply for Senior Secondary (Class 12)
- Pursue vocational education
- Apply for various training programmes
- Continue their educational journey through recognized pathways

<Callout type="info">
  The NIOS Secondary Certificate is recognized and serves as the foundation for
  further studies, including admission to the Senior Secondary programme.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Is the NIOS Secondary Programme equivalent to Class 10?">
  Yes. The Secondary Programme is equivalent to Class 10.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can adults enroll in the Secondary Programme?">
  Yes. NIOS does not have an upper age limit for admission.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pursue Senior Secondary after completing NIOS Secondary?">
  Yes. The Secondary Certificate can be used to meet the educational requirement
  for Senior Secondary admission.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do all subjects have practical examinations?">
  No. Practical requirements depend on the subjects selected.
</Accordion>
