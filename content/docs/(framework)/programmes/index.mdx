The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) offers a range of programmes designed to meet the needs of learners from different educational and professional backgrounds.

Whether you're looking to complete secondary education, pursue higher studies, or develop practical job-oriented skills, NIOS provides flexible learning pathways for learners of all ages.

### Secondary (Class 10)

- Equivalent to Class 10
- Minimum age: 14 years
- Flexible learning environment
- Foundation for further education

</Tab>

<Tab value="Senior Secondary">

### Senior Secondary (Class 12)

- Equivalent to Class 12
- Requires Secondary qualification
- Supports higher education pathways
- Suitable for competitive exam aspirants

</Tab>

<Tab value="Vocational">

### Vocational Education

- Skill-oriented learning
- Career-focused programmes
- Practical training and assessments
- Suitable for upskilling and employability

</Tab>

## Which Programme Should You Choose?

| If You Want To...                 | Consider                      |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Complete Class 10                 | Secondary Programme           |
| Complete Class 12                 | Senior Secondary Programme    |
| Prepare for college or university | Senior Secondary Programme    |
| Learn practical job-ready skills  | Vocational Education          |
| Continue education after a break  | Secondary or Senior Secondary |
| Improve career-related skills     | Vocational Education          |
