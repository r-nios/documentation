Admission fees are paid at the time of registration and vary depending on the course, stream, learner category, and number of subjects selected.

This page covers the admission fee structure for NIOS learners, including Stream 1 admissions, Stream 2/3/4 admissions, Transfer of Credit (TOC) fees, additional subject fees, and late admission charges.

<Callout type="info">
  The fee structure is based on the latest information available on the NIOS
  Student Portal. Fees may be revised by NIOS from time to time.
</Callout>

## Stream 1 Admission Fees

Stream 1 is intended for fresh admissions to Secondary and Senior Secondary courses.

### Secondary Course

| Category                   | Fee for 5 Subjects |
| -------------------------- | -----------------: |
| Male                       |             ₹2,340 |
| Female                     |             ₹1,890 |
| SC/ST, Ex-Servicemen & PwD |             ₹1,560 |

<Callout type="idea">
  <strong>Additional Subject Fee:</strong> ₹940 per subject.
</Callout>

### Senior Secondary Course

| Category                   | Fee for 5 Subjects |
| -------------------------- | -----------------: |
| Male                       |             ₹2,600 |
| Female                     |             ₹2,150 |
| SC/ST, Ex-Servicemen & PwD |             ₹1,690 |

<Callout type="idea">
  <strong>Additional Subject Fee:</strong> ₹940 per subject.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  The above fees include registration fees, PCP fees (where applicable), and
  study material costs.
</Callout>

### Late Admission Fees

NIOS allows admissions beyond the normal admission period by charging a late fee.

| Admission Window        | Additional Fee |
| ----------------------- | -------------: |
| Normal Admission Period |    No Late Fee |
| First Late Window       |           ₹260 |
| Second Late Window      |           ₹520 |
| Third Late Window       |           ₹910 |

<Callout type="warning">
  Late fees are added on top of the normal admission fee. Completing admission
  during the regular admission period helps avoid unnecessary charges.
</Callout>

  <Card title="Transfer of Credit (TOC)">

  <Card title="Online Application Form">

<Callout type="idea">
  <strong>Transfer of Credit (TOC)</strong> allows eligible learners to transfer
  marks from recognized boards, reducing the number of subjects they need to
  appear for under NIOS.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  An additional <strong>₹70</strong> is charged as the cost of the online
  application form for Stream 1 admissions.
</Callout>

## Stream 2, 3 & 4 Admission Fees

Streams 2, 3, and 4 are designed for learners seeking admission under special circumstances, additional qualifications, or Transfer of Credit provisions.

| Particular                      |              Fee |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------: |
| Registration Fee                | ₹620 per subject |
| TOC Fee                         | ₹230 per subject |
| Change / Additional Subject Fee | ₹940 per subject |

<Callout type="info">
  For Stream 2, 3, and 4 admissions, an additional <strong>₹50</strong> is
  charged as the online application form fee.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  Most Stream 2, 3, and 4 fees are calculated on a per-subject basis rather than
  a fixed admission fee.
</Callout>

## Fee Concession Categories

Learners belonging to the following categories are eligible for concessional admission fees:

- Scheduled Castes (SC)
- Scheduled Tribes (ST)
- Ex-Servicemen
- Persons with Disabilities (PwD)

<Callout type="idea">
  Upload the required supporting documents during admission to claim fee
  concessions. Failure to provide valid proof may result in concession benefits
  being denied.
</Callout>

### Documents Required for Fee Concessions

| Category      | Required Document                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| SC/ST         | Valid caste certificate issued by a competent authority |
| Ex-Servicemen | Certificate issued by the employer/competent authority  |
| PwD           | Disability certificate issued by a Government Hospital  |

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Admission fees and examination fees are separate. Paying admission fees does
  not automatically register you for Public Examinations or On-Demand
  Examinations.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Examination fees, practical fees, correction fees, and document-related
  service charges are payable separately whenever applicable.
</Callout>
