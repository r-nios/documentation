NIOS fees are generally non-refundable once a payment has been successfully processed. However, certain situations may be considered for refunds in accordance with applicable NIOS rules and policies.

This page provides a general overview of refund-related provisions and important considerations for learners.

<Callout type="warning">
  Before making any payment, carefully verify all details including personal
  information, course level, subjects, and payment amount. Most fees paid to
  NIOS are non-refundable.
</Callout>

## When Refunds Are Usually Not Allowed

Refunds are generally not permitted in the following situations:

- Change of mind after payment
- Incorrect subject selection by the learner
- Failure to appear for examinations
- Failure to submit Formative Assessments
- Missing admission or examination deadlines
- Voluntary withdrawal from NIOS
- Incorrect information provided by the learner during registration

<Callout type="idea">
  Learners should assume that payments are non-refundable unless a specific
  refund provision is mentioned by NIOS in an official notification.
</Callout>

## Situations That May Require Review

Certain exceptional circumstances may require examination by NIOS.

Examples may include:

- Duplicate payments for the same service
- Technical payment failures resulting in incorrect deductions
- Excess payments due to system or transaction issues
- Other cases specifically covered by official NIOS instructions

<Callout type="info">
  Refund eligibility, supporting documents, and processing procedures depend on
  the nature of the issue and the rules applicable at the time.
</Callout>

## Payment Issues

If a payment has been deducted but the transaction is not reflected on the NIOS portal:

1. Wait for the payment gateway and bank confirmation process to complete.
2. Check the admission or examination dashboard again after some time.
3. Keep the transaction ID and payment receipt ready.
4. Contact the appropriate NIOS support channel if the issue persists.

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not make repeated payments immediately after a failed transaction unless
  you are certain that the previous payment was unsuccessful.
</Callout>
