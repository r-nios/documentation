Examination fees are separate from admission fees and must be paid whenever a learner registers for Public Examinations or On-Demand Examinations (ODE).

Since NIOS conducts examinations through mulideale modes, the applicable fee depends on the type of examination being chosen.

<Callout type="warning">
  Paying admission fees does not automatically register you for examinations.
  Examination fees must be paid separately during the examination registration
  period.
</Callout>

## Public Examination Fees

Public Examinations are conducted twice a year by NIOS and are the most common examination mode used by learners.

| Component         |                                   Fee |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------: |
| Theory Subject    |                      ₹300 per subject |
| Practical Subject | ₹150 additional per practical subject |

<Callout type="idea">
  If a subject contains both theory and practical components, both fees are
  payable. For example, a practical subject requires the theory fee plus the
  practical fee.
</Callout>

### Example Fee Calculation

| Registration            | Total Fee |
| ----------------------- | --------: |
| 5 Theory Subjects       |    ₹1,500 |
| 5 Theory + 1 Practical  |    ₹1,650 |
| 5 Theory + 2 Practicals |    ₹1,800 |

## Late Fee Schedule

If examination registration is not completed during the normal registration window, additional charges are applicable.

### Normal Registration Period

<Callout type="info">
  No late fee is charged during the normal registration period.
</Callout>

## Registration Windows

| Registration Phase  | Typical Duration | Fee Structure     |
| ------------------- | ---------------: | ----------------- |
| Normal Registration |         ~31 Days | No late fee       |
| First Late Window   |         ~10 Days | ₹150 per subject  |
| Final Late Window   |         ~10 Days | ₹1600 per learner |

<Callout type="warning">
  Registration dates vary for each examination session. Always refer to the
  latest NIOS examination notification for the current registration schedule.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  NIOS usually provides a normal registration period followed by two late
  registration windows. The first late window charges an additional fee per
  subject, while the final window charges a consolidated late fee per learner.
</Callout>

## On-Demand Examination (ODE) Fees

The On-Demand Examination (ODE) system allows eligible learners to appear for examinations throughout the year based on seat availability.

<Callout type="info">
  ODE follows a separate fee structure from Public Examinations. Fees may vary
  based on the latest NIOS ODE notification.
</Callout>

<Cards>
  <Card title="Flexible Scheduling">
    Learners can choose available examination dates instead of waiting for the
    next public examination session.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Separate Fee Structure">
    ODE fees are different from Public Examination fees.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="idea">
  Before applying for ODE, verify the latest fee structure and subject
  availability on the official NIOS ODE portal.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Examination fees are generally non-refundable once examination registration
  has been successfully completed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Always download and save your payment receipt after successful fee payment.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  Learners appearing for additional subjects, improvement examinations, or
  re-attempts must pay examination fees separately for each registration.
</Callout>
