NIOS provides specific deadlines for admissions, examination registration, and other learner services. Missing these deadlines may result in additional fees, restricted services, or the inability to complete a process during the current session.

This page explains the most common late fees and penalties that learners may encounter.

<Callout type="warning">
  Late fees are charged in addition to the normal fee. Missing deadlines can
  significantly increase the total amount payable.
</Callout>

## Admission Late Fees

For Stream 1 admissions, NIOS usually provides a normal admission period followed by late admission windows.

| Registration Phase | Fee Structure |
| ------------------ | ------------- |
| Normal Admission   | No late fee   |
| First Late Window  | ₹260          |
| Second Late Window | ₹520          |
| Final Late Window  | ₹910          |

<Callout type="idea">
  NIOS generally provides multiple admission windows before closing admissions
  for a particular block. Learners who apply later pay progressively higher
  fees.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Admission schedules vary by block and session. Always check the latest NIOS
  admission notification for exact dates.
</Callout>

## Examination Registration Late Fees

Learners who miss the normal examination registration period may still be able to register by paying additional charges.

| Registration Phase  | Fee Structure     |
| ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Normal Registration | No late fee       |
| First Late Window   | ₹150 per subject  |
| Final Late Window   | ₹1600 per learner |

<Callout type="idea">
  Examination registration usually remains open for a short period after the
  normal deadline. The applicable late fee depends on the registration window.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Examination registration dates change every session. Always verify the current
  schedule before planning your registration.
</Callout>

## Common Situations That Lead to Penalties

Learners may incur additional costs when:

- Admission is completed after the normal registration period.
- Examination fees are paid after the normal registration deadline.
- Additional subjects are requested after admission.
- Duplicate academic documents are required.
- Corrections require paid learner services.

<Callout type="idea">
  Most penalties are not disciplinary in nature. They are administrative charges
  associated with late registration or additional services.
</Callout>

### How to Avoid Late Fees

<Cards>
  <Card title="Track Deadlines">
    Monitor admission and examination notifications regularly.
  </Card>

<Card title="Register Early">
  Complete registration during the normal window whenever possible.
</Card>

<Card title="Keep Documents Ready">
  Delays often occur because required documents are unavailable.
</Card>

  <Card title="Save Payment Proof">
    Maintain copies of receipts and transaction references.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="tip">
  The easiest way to avoid penalties is to complete admissions and examination
  registrations as soon as the registration window opens instead of waiting for
  the final days.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Late fees are generally non-refundable once the registration process has been
  completed successfully.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Fee structures, registration schedules, and penalty amounts may be revised by
  NIOS through official notifications.
</Callout>
