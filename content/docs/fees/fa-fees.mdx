Formative Assessment (FA) fees are not applicable to most learners.

The FA PCP fee is only meant for eligible learners who need to reappear in Formative Assessment (FA) practical sessions after missing them during their first year of admission.

<Callout type="info">
  This page is based on [Notification No.
  97/2023](https://www.nios.ac.in/media/documents/notification/yr2023/Admission/Notification-97-2023.pdf)
  dated 10 October 2023 regarding the facility of reappearing in Formative
  Assessment (FA), along with [Corrigendum No.
  66/2025](https://www.nios.ac.in/media/documents/notification/yr2025/Misc/corrigendum-66-2025.pdf)
  dated 14 January 2025.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Formative Assessment (FA) is applicable only to academic subjects with
  practical components. It does not apply to vocational subjects integrated with
  academic courses.
</Callout>

<Card title="Reappearance Only">

## Who Needs to Pay FA PCP Fees?

You need to pay FA PCP fees only if:

- You are a Stream 1 learner.
- Your 5-year admission validity period has not expired.
- You missed the FA PCP sessions in the first year of admission.
- The subject contains a practical component.
- You are applying for the FA reappearance facility.

<Callout type="idea">
  As per [Notification No.
  97/2023](https://www.nios.ac.in/media/documents/notification/yr2023/Admission/Notification-97-2023.pdf),
  the FA reappearance facility is available only to eligible Stream 1 learners
  who missed FA PCP sessions in the first year of admission and whose admission
  validity period has not expired.
</Callout>

### Who Does Not Need to Pay?

You do not need to pay FA PCP fees if:

- You have already completed FA successfully.
- You have already been awarded FA marks.
- Your subject does not contain a practical component.
- You are enrolled in vocational subjects where FA is not applicable.
- You are not eligible for FA reappearance.

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners who have already been awarded FA marks are not permitted to improve
  or reappear for FA marks.
</Callout>

## Important Fee Rule

<Callout type="info">
  As clarified through [Corrigendum No.
  66/2025](https://www.nios.ac.in/media/documents/notification/yr2025/Misc/corrigendum-66-2025.pdf),
  learners reappearing in Formative Assessment (FA) must pay the prescribed FA
  examination fee for each practical subject. This fee is payable in addition to
  the normal examination fee for the subject.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  There is no separate practical fee for FA during the first year. The FA PCP
  fee is applicable only when an eligible learner reappears in FA.
</Callout>

### Added a New Practical Subject?

Learners who add a new subject containing a practical component after their first examination may also be required to pay FA PCP fees for that subject.

<Callout type="idea">
  Since the learner did not attend the original FA PCP sessions for the newly
  added practical subject, FA PCP fees may become applicable for that subject
  along with the examination fee.
</Callout>

### Can FA Marks Be Improved?

No.

If FA marks have already been awarded, the learner cannot improve or reappear for Formative Assessment.

Only learners marked absent in FA may be eligible for the FA reappearance facility.

<Callout type="warning">
  Practical (SA) and theory marks may be improved through future examinations,
  but FA marks cannot be improved once awarded.
</Callout>

## Passing Year Impact

If new FA marks are added later:

- The passing year remains unchanged if the learner was already declared passed.
- If the learner becomes PASS only after adding FA marks, the passing year may change according to the current examination session.

<Callout type="idea">
  The passing year changes only when the newly awarded FA marks result in the
  learner being declared PASS for the first time.
</Callout>

## How to Apply for FA PCP?

FA PCP fees are paid through the Student Portal at the same time as examination fees.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Student Portal">
    Apply through the FA PCP option available on the learner dashboard.
  </Card>

<Card title="Along with Exam Fees">
  FA PCP fees are paid together with examination fees.
</Card>

<Card title="One-Time Facility">
  The FA reappearance facility can only be availed once during the 5-year
  admission validity period.
</Card>

  <Card title="Hall Ticket Required">
    A separate hall ticket is issued before FA PCP sessions.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="info">
  Hall tickets are generally issued separately for FA PCP sessions, Practical
  Examinations (SA), and Theory Examinations.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  No additional payment is required at the Study Centre for attending FA PCP
  sessions after the applicable FA PCP fee has been paid through the Student
  Portal.
</Callout>
