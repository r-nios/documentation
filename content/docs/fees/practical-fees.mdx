Certain NIOS subjects include practical examinations in addition to theory examinations. Learners appearing in these subjects must pay practical fees separately while registering for examinations.

<Callout type="info">
  Practical fees are charged in addition to the theory examination fee and are
  applicable only to subjects that include a practical component.
</Callout>

## Fee Highlights

<Cards>
  <Card title="Public Examination">
    ₹150 per practical subject.
  </Card>

<Card title="On-Demand Examination (ODE)">₹200 per practical subject.</Card>

<Card title="Theory Fee Separate">
  Practical fees do not include theory examination fees.
</Card>

  <Card title="Applicable Subjects">
    Only subjects with practical components require practical fees.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Public Examination Practical Fees

| Component                 |              Fee |
| ------------------------- | ---------------: |
| Practical Examination Fee | ₹150 per subject |

<Callout type="idea">
  Subjects containing both theory and practical components require payment of
  both the theory examination fee and the practical examination fee.
</Callout>

### Example

| Registration  | Amount Payable |
| ------------- | -------------: |
| Theory Fee    |           ₹300 |
| Practical Fee |           ₹150 |
| Total         |           ₹450 |

## On-Demand Examination (ODE) Practical Fees

| Component                 |              Fee |
| ------------------------- | ---------------: |
| Practical Examination Fee | ₹200 per subject |

<Callout type="idea">
  ODE practical fees are separate from Public Examination practical fees and are
  charged according to the ODE fee structure.
</Callout>

## Common Practical Subjects

Practical fees are generally applicable to subjects that include laboratory work, experiments, projects, or practical assessments.

Examples include:

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Home Science
- Data Entry Operations
- Painting
- Other practical-based vocational subjects

<Callout type="idea">
  Not sure whether your subject has a practical examination? Check the
  <a href="/docs/academics/subjects-syllabus">Subjects</a> section for detailed
  information about subject groups, practical components, marks distribution,
  and examination requirements.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  The practical fee is payable even if the learner is appearing only for the
  practical component of a subject.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="info">
  Practical examination schedules are announced separately from theory
  examination schedules.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Failure to appear for a practical examination does not automatically entitle a
  learner to a fee refund.
</Callout>
