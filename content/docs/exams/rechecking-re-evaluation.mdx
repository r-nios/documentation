NIOS provides learners with multiple options to address result-related concerns after examination results are declared. Depending on the nature of the issue, learners may apply for **Rechecking**, **Re-evaluation**, or **Result Correction** through the NIOS online portal.

## Rechecking & Re-evaluation [toc]

### Overview

These services help ensure that marks have been correctly awarded and recorded.

- **Rechecking** verifies totaling and clerical errors.
- **Re-evaluation** allows a fresh assessment of answer scripts.
- **Result Correction** is available for missing marks or incorrect result status.

## Difference Between Rechecking & Re-evaluation

| Process           | Available For                | Purpose                                                                        |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Rechecking**    | Secondary & Senior Secondary | Verification of totaling errors, unchecked answers, and mark transfer mistakes |
| **Re-evaluation** | Senior Secondary Only        | Fresh evaluation of theory answer scripts                                      |

## Eligibility

### Rechecking

- Available for Secondary and Senior Secondary learners.
- Can be applied for one or more subjects.

### Re-evaluation

- Available only for Senior Secondary learners.
- Applicable only to theory examinations.
- Not available for practical examinations.

## Re-evaluation Rules

### Eligibility & Scope

- Only learners who have appeared in the **Senior Secondary Examination** may apply for re-evaluation.
- Re-evaluation is available only for **theory papers**.
- Re-evaluation is **not available for practical examinations**.
- Learners may apply for one or more subjects.

### Application & Fees

- Applications must be submitted within **15 days** of result declaration.
- The prescribed fee must be paid online through the available payment methods.

### Award of Revised Marks

- If the increase in marks is **less than 5%** of the maximum marks, the original marks will normally remain unchanged.
- If the revised marks enable the learner to pass, the re-evaluated marks may be awarded.
- If the increase in marks is **5% or more** of the maximum marks, the revised marks will be awarded.
- If re-evaluated marks are lower than the original marks, the original marks will remain valid.

### Result & Marksheet

- Re-evaluation results are published on the NIOS website.
- Where applicable, a revised marksheet is issued after surrendering the previous marksheet to the concerned Regional Centre.
- Answer books are not shown to learners, and re-evaluation is not conducted in their presence.

### Finality of Decision

- The result of re-evaluation is final and binding.
- No further request for re-evaluation will be entertained.
- Any dispute regarding interpretation of these rules shall be decided by NIOS, whose decision shall be final.

## Result Correction

Result Correction is available when marks or examination records are missing or incorrectly reflected in the published result.

<Callout type="info">
  Result Correction requests must be submitted within 30 days from the
  declaration of Public Examination results.
</Callout>

### Available Correction Types

<Steps>
  <Step>

    **Practical Marks Not Included**

    Apply when practical marks are missing from the published result.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **TMA Marks Not Included**

    Apply when Tutor Mark Assignment (TMA) marks are not reflected in the result.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **TOC Marks Not Included**

    Apply when Transfer of Credit (TOC) marks have not been included.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Marked Absent in Theory**

    Apply if you appeared for the examination but have been incorrectly marked absent.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Result Correction Fee

| Service           | Fee                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| Result Correction | ₹50 per correction request |

<Callout type="warning">
  A non-refundable online transaction fee of ₹70 will be charged in addition to
  the prescribed fee.
</Callout>

### How to Apply for Result Correction

<Steps>
  <Step>

    **Login**

    Sign in to the NIOS Student Portal.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Open Result Correction**

    Click the **Apply** option under the Result Correction section.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Select Subject**

    Choose the subject for which correction is required.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Choose Correction Type**

    Select the appropriate correction category.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Pay Fee**

    Complete the online payment of ₹50 for each correction request.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Track Status**

    Use the **My Request** section to monitor the status of your application and download payment receipts.

  </Step>
</Steps>
