# FAQs & Common Misconceptions [toc]

This page answers some of the most common questions and misconceptions that
learners have about Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA).

## Quick Answers

## Is TMA Mandatory?

Not necessarily.

Learners can still appear for examinations even if they do not submit their
TMA. However, submitting TMA is strongly recommended because TMA marks
contribute towards the final evaluation process.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you do not submit a TMA, you will generally be marked
  <strong>AB (Absent)</strong> in the TMA component and will not receive marks
  for that portion of the evaluation.
</Callout>

## Can I Skip TMA?

Yes.

However, skipping TMA means you will not receive any marks for the TMA
component of that subject.

Although your examination eligibility is generally unaffected, your final
result may be impacted because you are effectively giving up marks that could
have contributed towards your overall score.

## Will My Result Be Withheld If I Don't Submit TMA?

No.

Your result is generally not withheld simply because you did not submit TMA.

However, the TMA component may show **AB (Absent)** and no marks will be
awarded for that portion of the evaluation.

## Is TMA the Same as Formative Assessment (FA)?

No.

TMA and Formative Assessment (FA) are completely different assessment
components.

| TMA                              | Formative Assessment (FA)             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Home-based assignment            | Practical assessment component        |
| Submitted through SDMIS          | Conducted through assessment sessions |
| Theory-related evaluation        | Practical-related evaluation          |
| Contributes to theory assessment | Contributes to practical assessment   |

<Callout type="info">
  Learn more about [Formative Assessment
  (FA)](/docs/exams/formative-assessment).
</Callout>

## Can I Copy Answers From Someone Else?

You should prepare your TMA using your own understanding and study materials.

Study Centres may reject assignments that appear incomplete, copied, or do not
follow the prescribed instructions.

## Can I Submit TMA After the Deadline?

Late submission acceptance depends on applicable NIOS guidelines.

Learners should always attempt to submit TMA within the prescribed submission
window.

## Can I Submit TMA Online?

Yes.

TMA is submitted through the **SDMIS Portal**.

For detailed instructions, see the
[Submission Process](/docs/exams/tutor-marked-assignments-tma/submission-process) guide.

## I Uploaded the Wrong File. What Should I Do?

If you uploaded:

- The wrong subject
- The wrong medium/language
- An incomplete assignment
- A corrupted PDF
- The wrong file entirely

you should correct the mistake as soon as possible.

<Callout type="warning">
  Once TMA marks have been evaluated and uploaded by the Study Centre, they
  generally cannot be edited or changed.
</Callout>

### Re-upload Within 7 Days

Learners are generally allowed to re-upload TMA free of cost within **7 days**
of the initial upload.

### After 7 Days

Use the TMA Re-upload service available through NIOS e-Services, if available.

If the service is unavailable, contact your Study Centre immediately for
guidance.

## Can I Improve My TMA Marks?

Generally, no.

NIOS does not provide a dedicated TMA improvement facility similar to
Improvement Examinations.

Once TMA marks have been evaluated and uploaded, they are generally treated as
final.

## Can I Check My TMA Marks Before Results?

TMA marks are not always available through a dedicated public portal.

Many learners only see the effect of TMA marks when results are declared.

## What Does "Public Exam Fees Not Paid" Mean?

Some learners encounter this message while trying to upload TMA.

This commonly occurs when a learner is attempting to upload TMA for a future
Public Examination session without first registering and paying examination
fees for that session.

<Callout type="info">
  In many cases, the issue is not related to the TMA itself. The system is
  checking whether the learner has registered for the relevant examination
  session.
</Callout>

## Do ODE Learners Need To Submit TMA?

This depends on the learner's stream, admission category, and applicable NIOS
rules.

Learners should refer to the latest guidelines or consult their Study Centre
for clarification.

## What Happens If My Study Centre Does Not Accept My TMA?

Contact the Study Centre immediately and keep records of:

- Assignment copies
- Uploaded PDF files
- Submission receipts
- Screenshots
- Communication with the Study Centre

These records may be useful if any submission-related issue arises later.
