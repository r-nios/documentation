# Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA) [toc]

Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA) are home-based assignments that learners must
complete and submit to their Study Centre for evaluation.

TMAs form an important part of the NIOS assessment system and contribute marks
towards the final result. They are designed to encourage continuous learning
and help learners engage with their subjects throughout the academic session.

<Callout type="warning">
  TMA marks are not optional. Learners should submit their assignments before
  the prescribed deadline to ensure their marks are considered during result
  processing.
</Callout>

## What is TMA?

A Tutor Marked Assignment (TMA) is a set of subject-specific questions provided
by NIOS that must be answered and submitted by the learner.

The assignments are evaluated by tutors or Study Centres and the awarded marks
are uploaded to NIOS.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Home-Based Assessment">
    Assignments are completed by learners outside the examination hall.
  </Card>

<Card title="Subject-Wise Submission">
  Separate assignments are generally required for each subject.
</Card>

<Card title="Tutor Evaluation">
  TMAs are assessed by the Study Centre and marks are uploaded to NIOS.
</Card>

  <Card title="Continuous Learning">
    Encourages regular study instead of relying entirely on examinations.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Key Highlights

| Feature             | Details                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Applicable To       | Stream 1 learners                    |
| Submission Mode     | Through the online                   |
| Evaluation          | By tutors/Study Centre               |
| Contribution        | Included in final result calculation |
| Subject Requirement | Separate assignment for each subject |

<Callout type="info">
  TMA is different from Formative Assessment (FA), Practical Examinations, and
  Theory Examinations. Each component serves a different purpose within the NIOS
  evaluation system.
</Callout>

## How TMA Fits Into the Assessment System

| Component                 | Purpose                                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| TMA                       | Home-based assignment work                           |
| Formative Assessment (FA) | Practical assessment component for eligible learners |
| Practical Examination     | Practical skills evaluation                          |
| Theory Examination        | Written examination                                  |

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Late submission may result in TMA marks not being considered for the relevant
  examination session.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Always keep a copy of your completed assignments and any submission receipts
  provided by the Study Centre.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  If you are unsure about TMA submission dates, assignment questions, or Study
  Centre requirements, contact your Study Centre directly.
</Callout>
