# Submission Process [toc]

Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA) are submitted through the **SDMIS Portal**.

After completing the assignment, learners must upload their TMA files through
the portal before the prescribed deadline.

<Callout type="warning">
  Submit your TMA before the deadline announced by NIOS. Late submissions may
  not be accepted.
</Callout>

## Before You Begin

Ensure that:

- Your TMA has been completed correctly.
- The pages are clear and readable.
- The assignment is scanned properly.
- The file format and size meet the portal requirements.
- The uploaded file is **5 MB or less**.
- You have your enrolment number and login credentials ready.
- You know the correct **medium/language** of your assignment.

<Callout type="warning">
  Make sure the uploaded TMA file is within the maximum file size limit allowed
  by the portal. In most cases, learners should keep the file size below
  <strong>5 MB</strong>.
</Callout>

## TMA Submission Steps

<Steps>
  <Step>

    Visit the SDMIS Portal and sign in using your learner credentials.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    Navigate to the **TMA Upload / TMA Submission** section.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    Select the subject and the correct medium/language of the TMA.

    Examples:

    i. English Medium → Upload English Medium TMA

    ii. Hindi Medium → Upload Hindi Medium TMA

    iii. Ensure the selected medium matches the language in which your TMA was
    completed.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    Upload the scanned TMA file in the prescribed format.

    Ensure:

     i. The file size is **5 MB or less**.

     ii. All pages are included.

     iii. The file is readable and correctly oriented.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    Verify the uploaded file carefully before submitting.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    Submit the assignment and wait for the confirmation message.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    Save a screenshot or copy of the submission confirmation for future reference.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## File Preparation Tips

<Callout type="info">
  Poor-quality scans may make evaluation difficult. Ensure all pages are
  properly visible before uploading.
</Callout>

Recommended practices:

- Scan all pages in the correct order.
- Ensure handwriting is readable.
- Avoid blurred photographs.
- Verify that every page is included.
- Check the uploaded file before final submission.

## After Submission

After successfully uploading the TMA:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Verify that the submission status is updated on the portal.
  </Step>

<Step>Keep a copy of the uploaded file.</Step>

<Step>
  Keep the original handwritten assignment until results are declared.
</Step>

  <Step>
    Monitor official notifications for any updates regarding evaluation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Correcting Mistakes After Submission

Once a TMA has been evaluated and marks have been uploaded by the Study Centre,
the marks generally cannot be edited, modified, or changed.

For this reason, learners should carefully review their uploaded assignment
before submission.

<Callout type="warning">
  TMA marks awarded by the Study Centre or evaluator are generally considered
  final. Once marks have been uploaded and processed, they cannot normally be
  edited or revised.
</Callout>

### Found a Mistake in Your Uploaded TMA?

If you discover that you uploaded:

- The wrong file
- An incomplete assignment
- A corrupted PDF
- Pages in the wrong order
- The wrong subject
- The wrong medium/language

you should correct the issue as soon as possible.

### Re-upload Within 7 Days

NIOS generally allows learners to re-upload their TMA free of cost within
**7 days of the initial upload**.

<Callout type="info">
  If you notice a mistake immediately after submission, do not wait until the
  evaluation process begins. Re-upload the corrected TMA as soon as possible.
</Callout>

### If the 7-Day Window Has Passed

If the free correction period has expired, learners may use the
**TMA Re-upload service** available through NIOS e-Services, if the service is
currently available.

### If E-Services Is Unavailable

If the re-upload service is unavailable or inaccessible for any reason, contact
your Study Centre (AI) immediately for guidance.

Your Study Centre may be able to advise you on the available options depending
on the status of the submission and evaluation process.

<Callout type="idea">
  Always keep copies of:
  
  - The original handwritten TMA
  - Uploaded PDF files
  - Submission confirmations
  - Screenshots of successful uploads

These records can be useful if any submission-related issue occurs later.

</Callout>

## Common Issues

### Upload Option Not Visible

The submission window may not be active yet, or the deadline may have passed.

### File Upload Failed

Check:

- File size limits
- Supported file format
- Internet connection

### Wrong Subject Selected

Contact the appropriate authority immediately if an incorrect subject was
selected during submission.

### No Confirmation Received

Always save screenshots of successful submissions for future reference.

### Incorrect Medium Selected

Selecting the wrong medium/language may result in submission issues or
evaluation delays.

Examples:

- Uploading an English TMA while selecting Hindi Medium
- Uploading a Hindi TMA while selecting English Medium

Always verify the selected medium before submitting.

### File Size Exceeds Limit

If the uploaded file exceeds the portal limit (typically 5 MB), the upload may
fail.

To reduce file size:

- Scan at an appropriate resolution.
- Compress the PDF if necessary.
- Remove unnecessary blank pages.
- Ensure images are clear but not excessively large.

### "Public Exam Fees Not Paid" Error

Some learners may see a **"Public Exam Fees Not Paid"** message while trying
to upload TMA through the SDMIS portal.

This usually happens when a learner has already registered for a previous
Public Examination session but plans to appear in the remaining subjects during
a later session.

#### Example

Suppose a learner registered for the **April–May 2026 Public Examination** with
5 subjects.

| Subject   | Status                               |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Subject 1 | Appeared                             |
| Subject 2 | Appeared                             |
| Subject 3 | Planned for October–November Session |
| Subject 4 | Planned for October–November Session |
| Subject 5 | Planned for October–November Session |

For the April–May session, TMA upload is generally allowed directly because the
system works that way.

However, when the learner later tries to upload TMA for the remaining subjects
for the October–November session same year or any future exams, the portal may display:

> Public Exam Fees Not Paid

This happens because the learner must first pay the Public
Examination fee for the new examination session before TMA upload becomes
available for those subjects.

<Callout type="info">
  In many cases, the issue is not related to TMA itself. The portal is checking
  whether the learner has registered for the relevant Public Examination
  session.
</Callout>

### Paid Examination Fees But Not Appearing?

Paying Public Examination fees does not force a learner to appear for the
examination.

For example:

- You paid examination fees for a subject.
- Later decided not to appear for that examination.
- You may simply skip the examination.

This generally does not create any issue.

### Important: Still Submit TMA

If you have already paid examination fees for a subject, it is strongly
recommended to upload the TMA for that subject even if you decide not to appear
for the examination.

<Callout type="warning">
  If examination fees have already been paid but TMA is not uploaded, learners
  may later be required to pay the TMA late submission fee (currently ₹1500)
  when attempting to submit TMA in a future session.
</Callout>

Therefore, if examination fees have already been paid, it is generally best to
submit the TMA even if you plan to postpone or skip the examination attempt.

<Callout type="idea">
  Keep copies of your TMA files, submission receipts, and screenshots until the
  final results are declared.
</Callout>
