# Improvement / Reappear Exams [toc]

NIOS provides learners with flexible opportunities to improve their academic performance and complete pending subjects. Through the **Improvement Examination** and **Credit Accumulation** system, learners can reappear in subjects without losing previously earned credits.

## Credit Accumulation

One of the most learner-friendly features of NIOS is its **Credit Accumulation System**.

Under this system, NIOS retains the credits earned in subjects that have already been passed. Learners can continue appearing in remaining subjects until all requirements for certification are fulfilled.

### Key Features

- Learners may avail multiple examination opportunities during the validity period of admission.
- Credits earned in passed subjects are retained.
- Passed subjects do not need to be repeated unless the learner wishes to improve marks.
- Certification is awarded after successfully clearing all required subjects.

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS conducts Public Examinations twice a year, allowing learners multiple
  opportunities to complete their certification requirements.
</Callout>

## Improvement Examination

An Improvement Examination allows a learner to reappear in a subject that has already been passed in order to obtain better marks.

### Eligibility

- The learner must have already passed the subject.
- Improvement is available within the 5 years validity period of admission.
- The prescribed examination fee must be paid.

### Important Rules

- The better of the two results is considered.
- Improved marks are reflected in the final record.
- Learners must follow the syllabus and examination scheme applicable to the examination session in which they appear.
- Part improvement is available only for Senior Secondary learners.

<Callout type="success">
  If the marks obtained in the improvement examination are higher than the
  previous marks, the improved marks will be reflected in the final record.
</Callout>

## Practical Subjects

For subjects containing practical examinations, additional conditions apply.

### Senior Secondary

- If a learner passes the Practical but fails the Theory component, the Practical marks are retained.
- The learner may reappear in Theory.
- If the learner chooses to appear in Practical again, the better result will be considered.

### Secondary

- Theory and Practical components are treated as a single unit.
- Learners reappearing in a subject must generally appear for both components as per the examination rules.

<Callout type="warning">
  Examination fees for both Theory and Practical components may still be
  applicable even if the learner does not intend to appear in one of the
  components.
</Callout>

## Improvement in TMA

Tutor Mark Assignments (TMA) follow separate rules.

<Callout type="warning">
  Improvement is not permitted in TMA marks. Assignments submitted after passing
  a subject will not be considered for improvement purposes.
</Callout>

## Improvement in Formative Assessment (FA)

Formative Assessment (FA) marks follow separate rules and cannot be improved after a subject has been successfully awarded.

<Callout type="warning">
  Improvement is not permitted in Formative Assessment (FA) marks. FA scores
  awarded for a subject remain final and are not eligible for improvement or
  re-assessment.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  For detailed information about Formative Assessment, submission guidelines,
  weightage, and evaluation rules, see the [Formative Assessment
  (FA)](/docs/exams/formative-assessment) page.
</Callout>

### For Certified Learners

Learners who have already obtained certification may also appear again for improvement examinations, subject to NIOS rules and eligibility conditions.

The revised marks, where applicable, will be reflected in the learner's academic records and supporting documents issued by NIOS.

<Callout type="info">
  For already certified learners appearing for improvement, the Marks
  Statement-cum-Certificate will be issued with the remark
  <strong>"Appeared for Improvement"</strong>.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="info">
  The better of the original and improvement examination results is normally
  considered for academic records and certification purposes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners appearing for improvement must pay the prescribed examination fee and
  follow the syllabus applicable to the examination session in which they
  appear.
</Callout>

<Callout type="success">
  Credit accumulation allows learners to complete their certification gradually
  without losing credits already earned in passed subjects.
</Callout>
