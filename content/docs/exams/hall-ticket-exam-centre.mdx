# Hall Ticket & Exam Centre [toc]

A **Hall Ticket** (Admit Card) is an essential document required to appear for
NIOS examinations and assessments. It contains important information such as
the examination date, reporting time, examination centre, subject details, and
instructions for learners.

Since NIOS conducts different types of examinations and assessments, learners
may receive different Hall Tickets depending on the activity they are
participating in.

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners must carry their Hall Ticket and a valid identity document while
  appearing for examinations. Entry may be denied if the required documents are
  not produced.
</Callout>

## Downloading Your Hall Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Complete your examination registration successfully. See
    [Exam Registration](/docs/exams/exam-registration).
  </Step>

<Step>
  Visit the official Hall Ticket portal when hall tickets are released by NIOS.
</Step>

<Step>

Enter your enrolment number and the required details.

</Step>

<Step>Download the Hall Ticket and verify all information carefully.</Step>

  <Step>
    Print or save a copy for use on examination day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Hall Tickets are generally released before the commencement of examinations.
  Learners should regularly monitor official NIOS notifications for updates.
</Callout>

### Verify Before Examination Day

Carefully verify the following details after downloading your Hall Ticket:

| Item                   | Details                                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Learner Name           | Ensure the name is correct.                            |
| Enrolment Number       | Verify that the enrolment number matches your records. |
| Subjects               | Confirm all registered subjects are listed correctly.  |
| Examination Dates      | Check the examination schedule carefully.              |
| Examination Centre     | Verify the allotted centre and venue details.          |
| Reporting Instructions | Read all examination-day instructions.                 |

<Callout type="warning">
  If any discrepancy is found in your Hall Ticket, contact the concerned NIOS
  authority immediately before the examination date.
</Callout>

## Examination Centre

The examination centre is the location where learners appear for theory
examinations.

<Cards>
  <Card
    title="Theory Examinations"
  >
    Theory examinations are conducted at the examination centre mentioned on the
    Hall Ticket.
  </Card>

  <Card
    title="Practical Examinations"
  >
    Practical examinations may be conducted at the Study Centre (AI) or another
    institution designated by NIOS.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="idea">
  Practical examination venues may differ from the theory examination centre.
  Learn more on the [Practical Exams](/docs/exams/practical-exams) page.
</Callout>

## Examination Day Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Reach the examination centre well before the reporting time.
  </Step>

<Step>
  Carry your Hall Ticket and a valid identity document.

**Accepted identity documents may include:**

Aadhaar Card,
PAN Card,
and Passport

  </Step>

<Step>Verify your examination room and seating arrangements.</Step>

<Step>
  Follow all instructions issued by invigilators and examination staff.
</Step>

  <Step>
    Do not carry prohibited items into the examination hall.
  </Step>
</Steps>
