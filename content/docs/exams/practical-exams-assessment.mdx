# Practical Exams (Assessment) [toc]

Practical Examinations assess a learner's practical knowledge, skills, and
ability to perform subject-specific activities. They are conducted for subjects
that contain practical components.

Depending on the learner's stream and examination mode, the practical
assessment structure may differ.

## Practical Exams at a Glance

<Cards>
  <Card title="Practical Subjects">

  </Card>

<Card title="Stream-wise Rules">

## Stream-wise Practical Assessment

### Stream 1 Learners

For eligible Stream 1 learners, practical marks are divided into two
components:

| Component                  | Weightage |
| -------------------------- | --------: |
| Formative Assessment (FA)  |       50% |
| Practical Examination (SA) |       50% |

For example, if a subject carries **20 practical marks**:

- 10 marks come from Formative Assessment (FA)
- 10 marks come from the Practical Examination (SA)

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 1 learners should also read the [Formative Assessment
  (FA)](/docs/exams/formative-assessment) guide because Practical Examinations
  contribute only 50% of the final practical marks.
</Callout>

### Stream 2 Learners

Stream 2 learners do not have a Formative Assessment component.

The entire practical score is awarded through the Practical Examination.

For example, if a subject carries **20 practical marks**, the full **20 marks**
are awarded through the Practical Examination.

### Stream 3 & Stream 4 Learners

Stream 3 and Stream 4 learners also do not have a Formative Assessment
component.

Their practical marks are awarded entirely through the Practical Examination.

<Callout type="info">
  Practical marks for Secondary and Senior Secondary courses may differ by
  subject. Refer to the relevant subject pages for exact practical mark
  distributions.
</Callout>

### ODE Learners

ODE learners do not have a Formative Assessment component.

The entire practical score is awarded through the ODE Practical Examination.

For example, if a subject carries **20 practical marks**, the full **20 marks**
are awarded through the ODE Practical Examination.

<Callout type="warning">
  Unlike Stream 1 learners, ODE, Stream 2, Stream 3, and Stream 4 learners do
  not receive practical marks through Formative Assessment.
</Callout>

## Practical Exam vs Formative Assessment

| Practical Examination (SA)                             | Formative Assessment (FA)                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Final practical examination                            | Continuous practical assessment                       |
| Conducted during the examination cycle                 | Conducted before the examination session              |
| Commonly known as the Practical Exam                   | Commonly known as 5 PCP or 5 Mandatory PCP            |
| Contributes 50% practical marks for Stream 1 learners  | Contributes 50% practical marks for Stream 1 learners |
| 100% practical marks for Stream 2/3/4 and ODE learners | Applicable only to eligible Stream 1 learners         |

<Callout type="idea">
  Looking for information about the 5 Mandatory PCP system? See [Formative
  Assessment (FA)](/docs/exams/formative-assessment).
</Callout>

## What is Assessed?

The exact assessment criteria vary by subject, but commonly include:

- Practical skills and techniques
- Laboratory or workshop activities
- Demonstration and performance
- Viva-voce (oral examination)
- Practical record files
- Project work where applicable
- Subject-specific practical tasks

## ODE Practical Examinations

Practical examinations for ODE learners are conducted separately from the
theory examination.

In most cases, learners appear for the ODE theory examination first and then
attend the practical examination during the same week, generally on **Friday**
or **Saturday**.

<Callout type="warning">
  NIOS generally does not issue a separate ODE Practical Hall Ticket.
</Callout>

Learners should contact their On-demand Examination Centre directly to obtain practical
examination dates, timings, and venue information.

### Who Follows This System?

- ODE learners
- Stream 3 learners appearing through ODE
- Stream 4 learners appearing through ODE

### Stream 2 Learners

Stream 2 practical examinations are generally conducted along with the regular
practical examination schedule followed by Stream 1 learners.

However, unlike Stream 1 learners, Stream 2 learners do not have a Formative
Assessment component and receive their practical marks entirely through the
Practical Examination.

## ODE Practical Marks for Stream 1 Learners

Stream 1 learners appearing through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system may
notice that the practical examination is conducted and assessed out of the full
practical marks.

For example, a Physics practical carrying **20 practical marks** may be
evaluated out of **20 marks** by the examiner during the ODE practical
examination.

However, during result processing, NIOS applies the Stream 1 practical
evaluation rules.

### Example

Suppose a Stream 1 learner appears for an ODE Physics practical and is awarded:

| Practical Exam Score | Maximum Marks |
| -------------------- | ------------: |
| 20                   |            20 |

Since Stream 1 practical marks are divided equally between Formative Assessment
(FA) and Summative Assessment (SA), NIOS processes the marks according to the
applicable evaluation scheme.

| Component                  | Marks Considered |
| -------------------------- | ---------------: |
| Formative Assessment (FA)  |               10 |
| Practical Examination (SA) |               10 |
| Total Practical Marks      |               20 |

<Callout type="info">
  Even if the practical examination itself was conducted out of the full marks,
  the final result is processed according to the Stream 1 practical assessment
  structure of 50% FA and 50% SA.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  This clarification applies specifically to Stream 1 learners appearing through
  ODE. Stream 2, Stream 3, Stream 4, and other learners without a Formative
  Assessment component receive their practical marks entirely through the
  Practical Examination.
</Callout>
