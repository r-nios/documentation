## Examination Lifecycle

Every learner typically progresses through the following stages:

| Stage         | Description                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Admission     | Enrolment into NIOS and subject selection        |
| Registration  | Applying for Public Exams or ODE                 |
| Hall Ticket   | Downloading examination admit card               |
| Assessment    | Theory exams, practicals, and TMAs               |
| Results       | Marks and grades declaration                     |
| Certification | Issuance of certificates and migration documents |

<Callout type="idea">
  Not all subjects follow the same assessment pattern. Some subjects include
  practical examinations and internal assessments in addition to theory papers.
</Callout>

## Important Information

<Callout type="warning">
  Admission alone does not register a learner for examinations. Examination
  registration must be completed separately within the prescribed registration
  period.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Examination schedules, registration dates, fee structures, and result
  timelines may change from one session to another.
</Callout>

## Where to Start?

If you are new to NIOS, begin with **Types of Exams** to understand whether
Public Examinations or the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system is applicable to
your situation.
