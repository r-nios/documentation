# Public Exams [toc]

Public Examinations are the primary examination system offered by NIOS. These examinations are conducted during scheduled examination sessions and are the most commonly chosen option by learners.

<Callout type="info">
  Public Examinations are conducted twice every year, typically during the
  April-May and October-November examination sessions.
</Callout>

### Key Features

<Cards>
  <Card title="Twice a Year">
    Public Examinations are generally conducted during the April–May and
    October–November examination sessions.
  </Card>

<Card title="Fixed Schedule">
  Learners register and appear according to the official examination calendar
  announced by NIOS.
</Card>

<Card title="Wide Availability">
  Available to eligible learners across all NIOS streams and subjects.
</Card>

<Card title="Theory & Practical Exams">
  Subjects may include theory papers, practical examinations, and other
  assessment components where applicable.
</Card>

<Card title="Multiple Subjects">
  Learners can appear in one or more registered subjects during an examination
  session.
</Card>

  <Card title="Recognized Certification">
    Successful completion contributes towards the award of NIOS certification.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Examination Process

| Stage         | Description                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Registration  | Register during the examination registration window.                                                                                            |
| Hall Ticket   | Download your admit card and verify examination centre details before the examination.                                                          |
| Examination   | Appear for theory papers and, where applicable.                                                                                                 |
| Evaluation    | Answer scripts, practicals, TMAs, and other assessment components are evaluated according to NIOS guidelines.                                   |
| Result        | Results and grades are published after the evaluation process is completed. See [Results & Grading System](docs/exams/results-grading-system).  |
| Certification | Successful learners become eligible for certificates and related documents. See [Certificates & Migration](/docs/exams/certificates-migration). |

## Examination Sessions

NIOS generally conducts Public Examinations in two sessions each year:

| Session        | Typical Period     |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| First Session  | April – May        |
| Second Session | October – November |

<Callout type="warning">
  Exact examination schedules, registration dates, and result declaration dates
  vary from session to session. Always refer to the latest NIOS notifications.
</Callout>

## Who Should Choose Public Exams?

Public Examinations are suitable for learners who:

- Prefer a structured examination schedule.
- Are preparing alongside a regular study plan.
- Do not require immediate examination opportunities.
- Wish to appear during standard NIOS examination sessions.

<Callout type="idea">
  Public Examinations are the default and most widely used examination mode for
  NIOS learners.
</Callout>
