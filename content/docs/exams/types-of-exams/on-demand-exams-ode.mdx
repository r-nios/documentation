# On-Demand Examinations (ODE) [toc]

The **On-Demand Examination (ODE)** system allows eligible learners to appear for examinations when they feel academically prepared, instead of waiting for the next Public Examination session.

ODE provides greater flexibility by allowing learners to choose available examination dates and centres based on seat availability.

<Callout type="info">
  ODE is designed to provide flexible examination opportunities throughout the
  year for eligible learners and designated subjects.
</Callout>

## ODE Schedule

NIOS generally conducts On-Demand Examinations during the following periods:

| ODE Window       | Examination Period | Session Mapping                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Winter ODE Cycle | January – March    | Counted under the previous year's October–November examination session |
| Summer ODE Cycle | June – September   | Counted under the current year's April–May examination session         |

<Callout type="warning">
  ODE examinations are generally not conducted during April, May, October, and
  November as these months are reserved for NIOS Public Examinations.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea">
  Although examinations may be taken in January, February, or March, they are
  administratively treated as part of the previous October–November examination
  session.
</Callout>

## Examination Centres & Schedule

| Component                   | Details                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Theory Examination Venue    | Conducted at designated Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) centres and the NIOS Headquarters at Noida.                                      |
| Theory Examination Days     | Generally conducted on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday at KVS centres. At NIOS Headquarters, examinations may also be conducted on Friday. |
| Theory Examination Timing   | Typically conducted from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.                                                                                                |
| Practical Examination Venue | Usually conducted at the learner's Accredited Institution (AI) / Study Centre or another institution designated by NIOS.                    |
| Practical Examination Days  | Generally conducted on Fridays and Saturdays, or as announced by NIOS.                                                                      |

<Callout type="info">
  Examination centres, timings, practical arrangements, and available slots may
  vary depending on the subject, region, and the latest NIOS notification.
</Callout>

## How ODE Works

| Stage          | Description                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Eligibility    | Verify that you meet the eligibility requirements for ODE.                                                                                             |
| Registration   | Register through the ODE system and pay the applicable examination fee. See [Exam Registration](/exams-assessments/exam-registration).                 |
| Slot Selection | Select an available examination date, subject, and examination centre based on seat availability.                                                      |
| Examination    | Appear for the examination on the allotted date and time. Practical examinations, where applicable, may be conducted separately.                       |
| Result         | Results are published after evaluation. See [Results & Grading System](/exams-assessments/results-grading-system).                                     |
| Certification  | Successful learners become eligible for certificates and related documents. See [Certificates & Migration](/exams-assessments/certificates-migration). |

## ODE vs Public Examinations

| Feature              | Public Examinations        | ODE                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Schedule             | Fixed examination sessions | Flexible examination dates       |
| Availability         | Twice per year             | January–March and June–September |
| Registration         | Session-based              | Slot-based                       |
| Flexibility          | Standard                   | Higher                           |
| Subject Availability | All eligible subjects      | Selected subjects only           |
| Seat Availability    | Not applicable             | Subject to available slots       |

### Who Should Choose ODE?

ODE may be suitable for learners who:

- Want greater flexibility in examination scheduling.
- Do not wish to wait for the next Public Examination session.
- Need an earlier opportunity to complete eligible subjects.
- Wish to improve their performance in eligible subjects.

<Callout type="warning">
  Not all subjects are available under ODE. Subject availability, examination
  centres, eligibility requirements, and examination schedules are determined by
  NIOS and may change over time.
</Callout>
