NIOS provides multiple examination options to accommodate different learning needs and schedules. Understanding each examination system helps learners choose the option most suitable for their academic goals.

<Callout type="info">
  Each examination type has its own eligibility requirements, registration
  process, examination schedule, and result timeline.
</Callout>

## Available Examination Types

<Cards>
  <Card
    title="Public Examinations"
    href="/exams-assessments/types-of-exams/public-examinations"
  >
    Scheduled examinations conducted during designated examination sessions.
  </Card>

  <Card
    title="On-Demand Examinations (ODE)"
    href="/exams-assessments/types-of-exams/on-demand-examinations"
  >
    Flexible examinations that allow eligible learners to appear throughout the
    year.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Understanding the Difference

| Public Examinations                   | On-Demand Examinations (ODE)                       |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed examination schedule            | Flexible examination dates                         |
| Conducted during examination sessions | Available throughout the year                      |
| Suitable for most learners            | Suitable for eligible learners seeking flexibility |
| Results declared after the session    | Results generally available sooner                 |

<Callout type="idea">
  Most learners appear through Public Examinations, while ODE offers additional
  flexibility for eligible candidates.
</Callout>
