# Exam Registration [toc]

Examination registration is the process through which NIOS learners apply to
appear in Public Examinations or On-Demand Examinations (ODE).

Registration for examinations is separate from admission and must be completed
within the prescribed registration period.

<Callout type="warning">
  Admission to NIOS does not automatically register a learner for examinations.
  A separate examination registration application must be submitted before
  appearing for any examination.
</Callout>

## Before You Register

Before starting the registration process, ensure that:

- Your NIOS admission is active.
- You have selected the subjects in which you wish to appear.
- You meet any eligibility requirements applicable to the examination.
- You have access to your enrolment number and login credentials.
- You are aware of the applicable examination fees.

<Callout type="info">
  Examination fees must be paid during registration. Learn more on the [Exam
  Fees](/docs/fees/exam-fees) page.
</Callout>

## Registration Process

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Login to the NIOS learner portal using your enrolment credentials.
  </Step>

<Step>
  Navigate to the [**Public Examination** tab](/images/public_exam_fees.png) and
  select the subjects for which you wish to appear.
</Step>

<Step>
  Review your personal details, subject selection, and examination information
  carefully.
</Step>

<Step>
  Pay the applicable examination fee using the available online payment methods.
</Step>

  <Step>
    Submit the application and save the registration confirmation and payment
    receipt for future reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Registration Windows

### Public Examinations

Public Examination registration is generally opened before each examination
session.

| Examination Session      | Typical Registration Period                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| April–May Session        | Announced by NIOS before the examination session |
| October–November Session | Announced by NIOS before the examination session |

<Callout type="idea">
  NIOS usually provides a normal registration period followed by late
  registration windows with additional fees.
</Callout>

### On-Demand Examinations (ODE)

ODE registration is available during the ODE examination cycle, subject to:

- Subject availability
- Examination centre availability
- Seat availability
- Eligibility requirements

See [On-Demand Examinations (ODE)](/docs/exams/types-of-exams/on-demand-exams-ode).

## After Registration

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Download and save the payment receipt generated after successful fee

<Step>
  Monitor official NIOS notifications and announcements for examination updates
  and important dates.
</Step>

  <Step>
    Download your Hall Ticket when it becomes available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Hall Tickets are issued separately after successful examination registration.
  See [Hall Ticket & Exam Centre](/docs/exams/hall-ticket-exam-centre).
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

<Cards>
  <Card title="Incorrect Subject Selection">
    Verify all selected subjects before submitting the application.
  </Card>

<Card title="Missing Registration Deadlines">
  Register within the normal registration period whenever possible.
</Card>

<Card title="Incomplete Fee Payment">
  Ensure the payment is successful and the receipt is saved.
</Card>

  <Card title="Ignoring Official Notifications">
    Check NIOS announcements regularly for schedule changes and updates.
  </Card>
</Cards>
