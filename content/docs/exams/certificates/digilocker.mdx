# DigiLocker Documents [toc]

NIOS provides digital academic documents through **DigiLocker**, the Government of India's secure digital document platform. Learners can access, download, and share their academic records online without waiting for physical copies.

## What is DigiLocker?

[DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) is a Government of India initiative that enables citizens to securely access official digital documents issued by various government departments, educational boards, and institutions.

For NIOS learners, DigiLocker serves as a convenient platform for accessing important academic documents anytime and anywhere.

<Callout type="info">
  Documents available in DigiLocker are digitally issued, digitally signed, and
  can be verified electronically by institutions, employers, and government
  agencies.
</Callout>

## Document Issuance Timeline

DigiLocker documents are not generated immediately after the declaration of results. NIOS first completes result processing, document generation, and synchronization with DigiLocker before the documents become available.

| Examination Type            | Typical DigiLocker Availability     |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Public Examination          | 10–20 days after result declaration |
| On-Demand Examination (ODE) | 30–45 days after result declaration |

<Callout type="info">
  Based on recent issuance trends, Public Examination documents are typically
  available within 10–20 days of result declaration, while ODE documents often
  take 30–45 days to appear in DigiLocker.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  These timelines are indicative and may vary depending on document generation,
  result corrections, technical synchronization, administrative processing, or
  other unforeseen delays.
</Callout>

## Benefits of DigiLocker

- Access documents anytime without visiting a Regional Centre.
- Download digital copies whenever required.
- Share verified academic records electronically.
- Reduce dependency on physical document storage.
- Securely store documents in a government-backed platform.
- Quickly retrieve documents during admissions, employment verification, and scholarship applications.

## Which Documents Are Physically Delivered?

| Document                           | Physical Copy | DigiLocker Copy |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate  | ✅ Yes        | ✅ Yes          |
| Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate | ✅ Yes        | ✅ Yes          |
| Passing Certificate                | ❌ No         | ✅ Yes          |

<Callout type="info">
  The standalone Passing Certificate is generally available through DigiLocker.
  Learners may not receive a separate physical copy of this document.
</Callout>

## How to Access NIOS Documents

### Existing DigiLocker Users

1. Sign in to your DigiLocker account.
2. Open the **Issued Documents** section.
3. Search for NIOS-issued academic documents.
4. View, download, or share the required document.

### New Users

1. Create a DigiLocker account using your mobile number.
2. Complete the required identity verification.
3. Sign in to your DigiLocker account.
4. Search for NIOS under the list of issuers.
5. Retrieve and download your academic documents.

## Physical Documents vs DigiLocker Documents

| Feature      | DigiLocker Documents           | Physical Documents                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Availability | Online                         | Delivered separately by NIOS           |
| Access Time  | Immediate after issuance       | Depends on printing and delivery       |
| Verification | Digital verification available | Manual verification                    |
| Storage      | Secure digital storage         | Physical storage required              |
| Sharing      | Easy electronic sharing        | Photocopies/scans required             |
| Re-download  | Available anytime              | Duplicate copy may require application |

## When Should You Use DigiLocker?

DigiLocker documents can be useful for:

- College and university admissions
- Scholarship applications
- Employment verification
- Government recruitment processes
- Document backup and safekeeping
- Online document submission
- Academic record verification

## Important Information

<Callout type="success">
  DigiLocker documents issued by NIOS are valid digital documents and are
  accepted for most admission, employment, and verification purposes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Ensure that your enrollment number, name, and date of birth are correctly
  recorded with NIOS. Incorrect details may prevent successful retrieval of
  documents from DigiLocker.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  If your examination result has been declared recently, wait until the document
  issuance cycle is completed before reporting missing documents.
</Callout>
