# Marksheet [toc]

The NIOS Marksheet is the official academic record issued to learners after the declaration of examination results. It contains subject-wise marks, grades, result status, and other important examination details.

Marksheets are issued both in **physical form** and **digital form through DigiLocker**.

### Overview

The marksheet serves as the primary academic document for NIOS learners and is commonly required for:

- College and university admissions
- Scholarship applications
- Employment verification
- Government recruitment processes
- Academic record verification

<Callout type="info">
  The NIOS marksheet is issued as a{" "}
  <strong>Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate</strong> for learners who
  successfully fulfill the certification requirements.
</Callout>

## How is the Marksheet Issued?

NIOS generally issues marksheets through the following channels:

| Method            | Availability                  |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- |
| DigiLocker        | Digital copy available online |
| Physical Dispatch | Printed copy sent by NIOS     |

### DigiLocker Copy

The digital marksheet can be accessed through DigiLocker once NIOS completes document generation and synchronization.

### Physical Copy

NIOS also issues a physical copy of the Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate after result processing, document generation, and printing.

#### Public Examination

For Public Examination learners, physical documents are generally dispatched to the **Accredited Institution (Study Centre)** associated with the learner's admission.

If the Study Centre has been closed, de-accredited, or is otherwise unable to receive documents, NIOS may dispatch the documents to the learner's **correspondence address** available in the NIOS records.

#### On-Demand Examination (ODE)

For ODE learners, the method of document delivery may vary between Regional Centres.

Depending on the Regional Centre's procedures:

- Documents may be dispatched to the learner's correspondence address.
- Learners may be required to collect the documents directly from the Regional Centre.
- Some Regional Centres may notify learners separately regarding document collection and delivery arrangements.

<Callout type="info">
  If you are an ODE learner, it is advisable to contact your Regional Centre for
  the latest information regarding the collection or delivery of physical
  documents.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Ensure that your correspondence address and contact details are up to date in
  NIOS records, as physical documents may be dispatched using the information
  provided during admission.
</Callout>

<Callout type="success">
  DigiLocker documents are digitally signed and are accepted by most educational
  institutions, employers, and government agencies.
</Callout>

## Marksheet Availability Timeline

The digital and physical copies of NIOS documents are usually made available at different times after the declaration of results.

### DigiLocker Availability

| Examination Type            | Typical Availability                |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Public Examination          | 10–20 days after result declaration |
| On-Demand Examination (ODE) | 30–45 days after result declaration |

<Callout type="info">
  DigiLocker documents are usually published before physical documents are
  received by learners.
</Callout>

### Physical Marksheet Delivery

| Document                          | Typical Delivery Timeline                         |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate | Approximately 30–45 days after result declaration |

<Callout type="info">
  Physical document delivery depends on printing, dispatch, postal transit,
  Study Centre handling, and Regional Centre processing.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Actual delivery timelines may vary depending on the examination session,
  document processing workload, postal services, and regional administrative
  procedures.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Based on recent issuance trends, Public Examination marksheets are usually
  available within 10–20 days of result declaration, while ODE marksheets often
  take 30–45 days to appear in DigiLocker.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Actual timelines may vary depending on result processing, corrections,
  document generation, and administrative requirements.
</Callout>

## If You Do Not Receive Your Marksheet

If your marksheet has not been received after a reasonable period:

1. Check whether the digital copy is available in DigiLocker.
2. Verify that your address details are correct in NIOS records.
3. Check for any pending result corrections or document processing.
4. Contact the concerned Regional Centre or NIOS support channels.

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners should not apply for duplicate documents immediately after result
  declaration. Physical dispatch may take additional time after documents become
  available in DigiLocker.
</Callout>

## Correction of Marksheet

If there is an error in the marksheet, such as:

- Name spelling mistakes
- Incorrect date of birth
- Missing marks
- Incorrect result status
- Other record-related discrepancies

the learner should apply through the appropriate NIOS correction process.

<Callout type="info">
  Result-related issues such as missing practical marks, missing Formative
  Assessment marks, missing TOC marks, or incorrect absentee status may be
  addressed through the Result Correction process.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS generally issues marksheets through both physical dispatch and
  DigiLocker. Digital copies often become available before the physical document
  is delivered.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  If there is an error in your marksheet, such as incorrect personal details,
  missing marks, or an incorrect result status, you should apply through the
  appropriate correction process as soon as possible.
</Callout>
