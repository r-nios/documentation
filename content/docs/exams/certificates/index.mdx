# Certificates [toc]

A NIOS certificate is awarded after a learner successfully completes the
required number of subjects and satisfies the applicable passing criteria.

Whether subjects are passed through **Public Examinations**, **On-Demand
Examinations (ODE)**, or a combination of both, the certification requirements
remain the same.

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS certificates issued through Public Examinations and ODE carry the same
  academic value and recognition.
</Callout>

## Certification Requirements

To receive a NIOS certificate, learners must successfully pass the minimum
required number of subjects prescribed for their course.

### Secondary Course (Class 10)

| Requirement               | Details                                  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Minimum Subjects Required | 5                                        |
| Certification Awarded     | Secondary Certificate                    |
| Language Requirement      | At least 1 and not more than 2 languages |

### Senior Secondary Course (Class 12)

| Requirement               | Details                                  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Minimum Subjects Required | 5                                        |
| Certification Awarded     | Senior Secondary Certificate             |
| Language Requirement      | At least 1 and not more than 2 languages |

<Callout type="warning">
  Passing fewer than five subjects does not make a learner eligible for
  certification, even if individual subjects have been successfully cleared.
</Callout>

## Official Pass & Certification Criteria

| Course                  | Pass Criteria                                                                                  | Certification Criteria                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Secondary Course        | Minimum 33% marks in aggregate (Theory + Practical where applicable)                           | Pass in 5 subjects including at least one but not more than two languages |
| Senior Secondary Course | Minimum 33% marks separately in Theory and Practicals (where applicable) and also in aggregate | Pass in 5 subjects including at least one but not more than two languages |

## Theory Passing vs Final Marks

Many learners assume that high TMA marks can compensate for low theory marks.

This is incorrect.

To pass a subject, learners must first satisfy the minimum theory passing
requirement.

<Callout type="warning">
  TMA marks are not considered when determining whether a learner has passed the
  theory component of a subject.
</Callout>

### Example 1

| Component          | Marks |
| ------------------ | ----: |
| Theory Examination | 25/80 |
| TMA                | 20/20 |

**Result:** ❌ Fail

Although the learner obtained full TMA marks, the theory score does not satisfy
the minimum passing requirement.

### Example 2

| Component          | Marks |
| ------------------ | ----: |
| Theory Examination | 30/80 |
| TMA                |  8/20 |

**Result:** ✅ Pass

The learner satisfies the minimum theory passing requirement.

## Credit Accumulation System

One of the most important features of NIOS is the **Credit Accumulation
System**.

NIOS retains the credits (passed subjects) earned by a learner and allows them
to accumulate passes across multiple examination sessions until certification
requirements are fulfilled.

<Callout type="info">
  Learners generally get up to five years from the date of admission to complete
  the required subjects and obtain certification.
</Callout>

### Example

| Session               | Subjects Passed      |
| --------------------- | -------------------- |
| April–May 2026        | English, Mathematics |
| October–November 2026 | Physics              |
| April–May 2027        | Chemistry, Biology   |

Result: ✅ Eligible for certification after completing the required subjects.

NIOS automatically retains the previously passed subjects and combines them for
certification.

## Public Examination & ODE Certification

NIOS allows learners to combine subjects passed through Public Examinations and
ODE for certification purposes.

### Example

| Subject     | Examination Type   |
| ----------- | ------------------ |
| English     | Public Examination |
| Physics     | ODE                |
| Chemistry   | Public Examination |
| Biology     | ODE                |
| Mathematics | Public Examination |

Result: ✅ Eligible for Certification

<Callout type="info">
  Certificates and marksheets do not indicate whether a subject was passed
  through Public Examination or ODE.
</Callout>

## Retention of Passed Components

For Senior Secondary learners, theory and practical components may be retained
separately in certain situations.

### Passed Practical but Failed Theory

If a learner passes the Practical Examination but fails the Theory
Examination:

- Practical marks are retained.
- The learner may reappear in Theory.

If the learner voluntarily appears in Practical again, the better of the two
practical performances may be considered.

### Passed Theory but Failed Practical

If a learner passes Theory but fails Practical:

- Theory marks are retained.
- The learner may reappear in Practical.

<Callout type="warning">
  Examination fees for both Theory and Practical components may still be payable
  during registration even if the learner intends to appear in only one
  component.
</Callout>

## Improvement of Performance

NIOS allows learners to improve performance in a subject that has already been
passed.

### Important Rules

- Improvement can generally be availed only once per subject.
- It must be availed within the validity period of admission.
- Examination fees must be paid again.
- For subjects having practicals, learners are generally required to appear in
  both Theory and Practical components.

<Callout type="info">
  The better of the two performances is normally retained and reflected in the
  marksheet.
</Callout>

## Senior Secondary Two-Year Gap Requirement

For certification at the Senior Secondary level, NIOS requires a minimum
two-year gap from the year of passing the Secondary examination.

### Example

If a learner passed Secondary in 2025 and seeks Senior Secondary
certification, the required two-year gap must be satisfied before full
certification eligibility is achieved.

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners who have not completed the required gap period may be restricted in
  the number of subjects they can appear for until the requirement is fulfilled.
</Callout>

## After Passing All Required Subjects

Once a learner successfully completes the certification requirements, NIOS
processes the learner's academic record and prepares the certification
documents.

This includes:

- Marksheet
- Provisional Certificate
- Migration Certificate
- DigiLocker Records

The document generation and dispatch process may take additional time after
result declaration.

## Document Delivery

The exact method of receiving documents depends on the examination mode and
Regional Centre.

### Public Examination Learners

Documents are generally dispatched to the learner's Study Centre (AI) for
collection.

### ODE Learners

Depending on the Regional Centre, documents may:

- Be collected from the Regional Centre.
- Be sent to the correspondence address.
- Be distributed through another designated office.
