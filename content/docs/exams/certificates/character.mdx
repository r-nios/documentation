# Character Certificate [toc]

A Character Certificate is a document commonly issued by regular schools and educational institutions to certify a student's conduct and behavior during their period of study.

## Does NIOS Issue Character Certificates?

<Callout type="warning">
  NIOS generally does not issue Character Certificates to learners.
</Callout>

As an **Open and Distance Learning (ODL)** institution, NIOS does not maintain continuous supervision of learners in the same manner as regular schools. Therefore, Character Certificates are not part of the standard document set issued by NIOS.

## Documents Issued by NIOS

Instead of a Character Certificate, NIOS issues academic documents such as:

  <Card

  <Card

## What If a College or Employer Requests a Character Certificate?

Some colleges, universities, employers, or government organizations may request a Character Certificate as part of their admission or recruitment process.

In such cases, learners should contact the concerned institution and explain that NIOS is an Open and Distance Learning institution and does not generally issue Character Certificates.

<Callout type="info">
  Many institutions accept NIOS academic documents such as the
  Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate and Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate in
  place of documents typically issued by regular schools.
</Callout>

## Other Documents Not Issued by NIOS

As an Open and Distance Learning (ODL) institution, NIOS does not issue certain documents that are commonly provided by regular schools.

<Callout type="warning">
  The following documents are generally not issued by NIOS:
</Callout>

| Document                         | Status        |
| -------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Bonafide Certificate             | ❌ Not Issued |
| Transfer Certificate (TC)        | ❌ Not Issued |
| School Leaving Certificate (SLC) | ❌ Not Issued |
| Character Certificate            | ❌ Not Issued |
| Date of Birth Certificate        | ❌ Not Issued |

<Callout type="info">
  Learners should use NIOS-issued documents such as the
  <strong>Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate</strong> and
  <strong>Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate</strong> wherever proof of
  education or certification is required.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners should verify document requirements directly with the institution,
  university, or employer requesting the Character Certificate.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Requirements may vary between institutions. Acceptance of alternative
  documents is at the discretion of the receiving organization.
</Callout>
