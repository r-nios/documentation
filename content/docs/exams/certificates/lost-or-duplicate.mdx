## Revision of Documents

If an issued document contains incorrect information, learners may apply for revision through the e-Services portal.

Revision services are commonly used for:

- Name corrections
- Parent's name corrections
- Date of birth corrections (subject to applicable rules)
- Other permitted document corrections
- Updating information in issued certificates and marksheets

<Callout type="warning">
  Revision requests are subject to NIOS rules, eligibility requirements, and
  supporting document verification.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  If a revision request is approved, the learner must surrender the original
  document to NIOS before the revised document is issued.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Revised documents are not issued alongside the old documents. The original
  marksheet, certificate, migration certificate, or other affected document must
  be returned to NIOS before the revised version can be released.
</Callout>

## How to Apply

Applications for duplicate documents and revision services can be submitted online through the NIOS e-Services portal.

<Steps>
  <Step>

    **Visit the e-Services Portal**

    Open the NIOS e-Services portal and sign in using your credentials.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Select the Required Service**

    Choose the document service you wish to apply for, such as a duplicate document or document revision.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Upload Supporting Documents**

    Upload all required documents and supporting evidence as specified for the selected service.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Pay the Applicable Fee**

    Complete the online payment for the requested service.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Submit the Application**

    Review the details carefully and submit the application.

  </Step>

  <Step>

    **Track Application Status**

    Monitor the progress of your application through the e-Services portal dashboard.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Processing times may vary depending on the service requested, document
  verification requirements, and administrative workload.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Ensure that all information and supporting documents submitted with your
  application are accurate and valid. Incorrect or incomplete applications may
  result in delays or rejection.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Duplicate documents and document revision services are processed through the
  NIOS e-Services portal and may require verification before approval.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">

  reference. Obtaining duplicate documents may require additional processing
  time and fees.
