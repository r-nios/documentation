# Migration [toc]

The **Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate (MTC)** is an official document issued by NIOS to eligible learners after successful certification. It serves as proof that a learner has completed their studies with NIOS and may be used when seeking admission to another educational institution.

### Overview

The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate combines the functions of both a **Migration Certificate** and a **Transfer Certificate** into a single document.

It is commonly required for:

- Admission to colleges and universities
- Transfer to another educational institution
- Academic record verification
- Higher education applications

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS generally issues a combined{" "}
  <strong>Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate</strong> rather than separate
  Migration and Transfer Certificates.
</Callout>

## Eligibility

A Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is generally issued to learners who have successfully completed the certification requirements of NIOS.

## Issuance Policy

The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is issued only once upon successful completion of certification requirements.

<Callout type="warning">
  The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is not re-issued after Improvement
  Examinations. Regardless of the number of improvement attempts or subsequent
  changes in marks, learners generally receive only one Migration-cum-Transfer
  Certificate.
</Callout>

## When Is It Issued?

The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is generally issued along with the learner's certification documents after successful completion of the course.

For most learners, the Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is generated and dispatched at the same time as the Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate.

<Callout type="info">
  Learners usually receive their Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate together
  with their Marksheet-cum-Passing Certificate during the document issuance
  process.
</Callout>

### Physical Copy

The physical certificate is generally issued along with other certification documents after completion of result processing and document printing.

<Callout type="warning">
  Physical delivery timelines may vary depending on document generation,
  dispatch schedules, postal services, and Regional Centre procedures.
</Callout>

### DigiLocker Availability

The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate generally becomes available in DigiLocker alongside other certification documents issued by NIOS.

| Examination Type            | Typical Availability                |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Public Examination          | 10–20 days after result declaration |
| On-Demand Examination (ODE) | 30–45 days after result declaration |

<Callout type="success">
  DigiLocker certificates are digitally signed and are generally accepted by
  educational institutions, universities, employers, and government agencies.
</Callout>

## Difference Between TOC and Migration Certificate

Many learners confuse **Transfer of Credit (TOC)** with the **Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate**.

| Transfer of Credit (TOC)              | Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate          |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Used during admission                 | Issued after certification                  |
| Transfers marks from a previous board | Serves as a migration and transfer document |
| Admission-related facility            | Certification-related document              |
| Applied separately                    | Issued after successful completion          |

<Callout type="warning">
  Transfer of Credit (TOC) and the Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate are
  separate services and should not be confused with each other.
</Callout>

## Duplicate Migration Certificate

If the original Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is lost, damaged, or unavailable, learners may apply for a duplicate copy through the NIOS e-Services portal by paying the prescribed fee and submitting the required documents.

<Callout type="info">
  Applications for duplicate certificates, marksheets, migration certificates,
  and other academic documents can be submitted online through the NIOS
  e-Services portal.
</Callout>

## Important Notes

<Callout type="info">
  The Migration-cum-Transfer Certificate is generally issued only after the
  learner has successfully completed the certification requirements of the
  respective course.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Ensure that your personal details, including name and date of birth, are
  correctly recorded in NIOS records before documents are generated.
</Callout>
