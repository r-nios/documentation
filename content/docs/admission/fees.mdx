# Admission Fees [toc]

NIOS charges admission fees at the time of registration. The total fee payable depends on factors such as the course selected, number of subjects, practical subjects, admission stream, and applicable admission charges.

This page covers only the fees associated with the admission process.

<Callout type="warning">
  Admission fees are revised periodically by NIOS. Always verify the latest fee
  structure through the official admission portal before making payment.
</Callout>

## What Is Included in the Admission Fee?

Depending on the selected course, subjects, and admission stream, the admission fee may include:

- Registration and admission charges.
- Subject fees.
- Practical subject fees (where applicable).
- Additional subject fees.
- Applicable late fees.
- Portal or processing charges, where applicable.
- Study Materials i.e Books

<Callout type="info">
  The exact fee payable is automatically calculated by the NIOS admission portal
  based on your selected course, subjects, stream, and applicable charges.
</Callout>

## What Is Not Included?

The admission fee only covers the admission process and related charges applicable at the time of admission.

The following fees are generally separate and are not included in the admission fee:

- Public Examination Fees
- On-Demand Examination (ODE) Fees (Stream 1 only not included)
- Rechecking / Re-evaluation Fees
- Duplicate Marksheet or Certificate Fees
- Migration Certificate Fees
- Correction Fees
- Transfer of Credit (TOC) Fees
- Other Student Service Fees

<Callout type="warning">
  For Stream 1 learners, payment of the admission fee does not automatically
  include future Public Examination fees. Examination registration and
  examination fees are generally paid separately when examination registration
  opens.
</Callout>

## Factors Affecting Admission Fees

| Factor             | Impact                                              |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Course Selected    | Secondary, Senior Secondary, or Vocational          |
| Number of Subjects | More subjects may increase fees                     |
| Practical Subjects | Additional practical fees may apply                 |
| Admission Stream   | Different streams may have different fee structures |
| Late Admission     | Additional late fees may be applicable              |

## Practical Subject Fees

Learners selecting practical subjects may be required to pay additional practical fees as prescribed by NIOS.

Practical fees generally cover:

- Practical examinations.
- Practical assessment activities.
- Practical-related administrative charges.

## Payment Methods

NIOS admissions are generally completed through online payment methods available on the admission portal.

Common payment options may include:

- Debit Cards
- Credit Cards
- Net Banking
- UPI
- Other payment methods supported by the portal

## Payment Guidelines

Before making payment:

- Verify all admission details.
- Confirm your selected subjects.
- Review uploaded documents.
- Ensure sufficient funds are available.
- Save payment confirmations and receipts.

<Callout type="tip">
  Always download and save the payment receipt after a successful transaction.
</Callout>

## After Payment

Once payment is successfully completed:

1. The application is submitted to NIOS.
2. Payment information is recorded.
3. The admission process moves to verification.
4. Admission status can be tracked through the portal.
