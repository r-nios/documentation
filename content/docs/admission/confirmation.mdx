# Confirmation Status [toc]

After submitting your admission application and successfully paying the admission fee, your application undergoes verification and approval before admission is confirmed.

This page explains how admission verification works, how to check your admission status, and what different status messages mean.

## How to Check Admission Status

NIOS provides multiple ways to check the current status of an admission application.

### Option 1 — Admission Status Portal

You can directly check your admission status using:

- Registered Email Address
- Reference Number
- Enrollment Number

## What Happens After Submission?

Once your application is submitted and payment is successfully completed, it goes through multiple stages of verification and approval before admission is confirmed.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Application Submission**

    Your application, uploaded documents, and payment details are submitted
    through the NIOS admission portal.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Regional Centre Verification**

    The application is forwarded to the concerned NIOS Regional Centre (RC) for
    verification.

    The Regional Centre reviews:

    - Personal details
    - Eligibility requirements
    - Uploaded documents
    - Subject selections
    - Supporting certificates

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Correction Request (If Required)**

    If any discrepancy, missing document, mismatch, or verification issue is
    found, the application may be returned for correction.

    Learners are generally notified through the admission portal and may also
    receive communication through their registered contact details.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Regional Centre Approval**

    If all documents and information are found satisfactory, the Regional Centre
    verifies the application and forwards it for further processing.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **NIOS Headquarters Review**

    The verified application is forwarded to the NIOS Headquarters (SSS
    Department) for final scrutiny and approval.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Admission Confirmation**

    Once approved by the competent authority, the admission is confirmed and
    learner services become available.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Admission approval generally involves verification by the concerned Regional
  Centre followed by final approval at NIOS Headquarters (SSS Department).
</Callout>

## Common Admission Status Messages

### Submitted

Your application has been successfully submitted and is awaiting verification.

### Under Verification

The Regional Centre is reviewing your application and uploaded documents.

### Correction Required

One or more details or documents require correction before verification can be completed.

### Verified by Regional Centre

The Regional Centre has successfully verified the application and forwarded it to NIOS Headquarters for further processing.

### Forwarded to Headquarters

The application has been forwarded to the NIOS Headquarters (SSS Department) for final scrutiny and approval.

### Admission Confirmed

The admission has been approved and learner services are available.

### Rejected

The application could not be approved due to eligibility issues, document problems, or other reasons.

<Callout type="warning">
  A rejection does not always mean permanent ineligibility. Carefully review the
  reason provided and follow the instructions issued by NIOS.
</Callout>

## Correction Required

Applications may be returned for correction if:

- Documents are blurry or unreadable.
- Wrong documents were uploaded.
- Name mismatch exists across documents.
- Date of birth mismatch is found.
- Educational qualification proof is missing.
- Supporting certificates are incomplete.

<Callout type="idea">
  Respond to correction requests as soon as possible to avoid delays in
  admission approval.
</Callout>

## How Long Does Verification Take?

Verification time may vary depending on:

- Admission volume.
- Document quality.
- Regional Centre workload.
- Additional verification requirements.

<Callout type="info">
  There is no fixed processing time applicable to every application. Some
  applications may be processed faster than others.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

### My payment was successful but the status has not changed.

Payment confirmation and admission verification are separate processes. Your application may still be awaiting verification.

### My application shows "Correction Required".

Review the correction remarks carefully and upload the required corrections through the prescribed process.

### My admission has been confirmed. What should I do next?

After admission confirmation:

1. Verify your subject selections.
2. Access study materials.
3. Contact your Study Centre (if applicable).
4. Begin academic preparation.

### My application was rejected.

Review the reason provided by NIOS and determine whether corrective action or a fresh application is required.
