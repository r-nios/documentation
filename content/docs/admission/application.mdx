# Admission Application [toc]

The NIOS admission process is conducted online through the official admission portal. Before applying, learners should understand the available admission streams, eligibility requirements, document requirements, and subject selection rules.

This page provides a general admission guide applicable to all NIOS admission streams.

## Which Stream Are You Applying Through?

Before starting your application, determine which admission stream applies to your situation.

| Stream   | Intended For                                                                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stream 1 | Fresh admission for Secondary or Senior Secondary learners.                             |
| Stream 2 | Learners with previous board examination records, failed candidates, or TOC applicants. |
| Stream 3 | Secondary (Class 10) admission through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.          |
| Stream 4 | Senior Secondary (Class 12) admission through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.   |

## General Admission Process

The overall admission process is broadly similar across all NIOS streams, although eligibility requirements, supporting documents, examination systems, and post-admission procedures may differ.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Choose the Appropriate Stream**

    Determine whether Stream 1, Stream 2, Stream 3, or Stream 4 applies to your situation.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Verify Eligibility**

    Review the age requirements, qualification requirements, and stream-specific eligibility conditions.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Prepare Required Documents**

    Gather the necessary identity documents, educational records, photographs, and supporting documents.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Complete the Online Application**

    Fill in personal details, educational details, and subject selections through the admission portal.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Upload Supporting Documents**

    Upload all required documents in the prescribed format.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Pay the Applicable Fee**

    Complete payment through the available online payment options.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Admission Verification**

    NIOS verifies the application and supporting documents.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Admission Confirmation**

    Once approved, learners can access study materials, examination services, and other learner support facilities.

  </Step>
</Steps>

| ----------------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Public Examination      | ✅          | ✅          | ❌           | ❌           |
| ODE System              | ❌          | ❌          | ✅           | ✅           |
| Fresh Admission         | ✅          | ❌          | ✅           | ✅           |
| TOC Eligibility         | ❌          | ✅          | ❌           | ❌           |
| Study Centre Allocation | Usually Yes | Usually Yes | Generally No | Generally No |
| PCP Availability        | Usually Yes | Usually Yes | Generally No | Generally No |

## Before You Apply

Before submitting your application, ensure that you:

- Have selected the correct admission stream.
- Meet the eligibility requirements.
- Have valid supporting documents available.
- Have a working mobile number and email address.
- Have reviewed the subject selection rules.
- Have access to an online payment method.

<Callout type="warning">
  Selecting the wrong admission stream may lead to admission complications,
  delays, or ineligibility under the chosen category.
</Callout>

## Information Required During Admission

During the admission process, learners are generally required to provide the following information:

| Category             | Examples                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Personal Details     | Name, Gender, Date of Birth            |
| Contact Information  | Mobile Number, Email Address           |
| Address Information  | Residential and Correspondence Address |
| Educational Details  | Previous Qualifications                |
| Subject Selection    | Course and Subject Choices             |
| Supporting Documents | Identity and Educational Documents     |

## Subject Selection

Selecting the correct subjects is one of the most important steps in the admission process.

When choosing subjects:

- Verify subject eligibility requirements.
- Review language requirements.
- Check practical subject requirements.
- Ensure that the chosen combination satisfies NIOS rules.

## Uploading Documents

When uploading documents:

- Use clear and readable scans.
- Ensure that all information is visible.
- Avoid cropped images.
- Verify that the correct document is uploaded in the correct category.
- Review all uploads before submission.

<Callout type="warning">
  Incorrect or unreadable documents may delay verification and admission
  approval.
</Callout>

## After Submission

After submitting your application:

- NIOS verifies the application.
- Uploaded documents are reviewed.
- Corrections may be requested if necessary.
- Admission status is updated on the portal.
- Learners gain access to academic and examination services after confirmation.

<Callout type="info">
  Admission verification times may vary depending on application volume,
  document quality, and verification requirements.
</Callout>
