# Subjects & Combinations [toc]

One of the most important parts of the NIOS admission process is selecting the appropriate subjects. NIOS offers a flexible subject selection system that allows learners to choose subjects according to their interests, academic goals, career plans, and eligibility requirements.

Unlike many traditional school systems, NIOS provides significant flexibility in choosing subject combinations.

## Subject Requirements

To obtain certification, learners must comply with the minimum subject requirements prescribed by NIOS.

| Requirement                 | Secondary | Senior Secondary |
| --------------------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| Minimum Subjects            | 5         | 5                |
| Maximum Subjects            | 7         | 7                |
| At Least One Language       | Yes       | Yes              |
| Additional Subjects Allowed | Yes       | Yes              |

<Callout type="info">
  Learners must choose at least one language subject and satisfy the minimum
  subject requirement prescribed by NIOS.
</Callout>

## General Subject Selection Rules

When selecting subjects, learners should generally ensure that:

- At least one language and maximum two language subject is selected.
- The minimum subject requirement is satisfied.
- The selected combination supports future academic or career goals.
- Practical subject requirements are understood before selection.
- Eligibility requirements for higher education or competitive examinations are considered.

## Choosing Subjects for Higher Education

Many universities and professional institutions may prescribe specific subject requirements for admission.

Examples include:

| Goal             | Common Subject Recommendations                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Engineering      | Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry                   |
| Medical Courses  | Biology, Physics, Chemistry                       |
| Commerce         | Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics          |
| Humanities       | History, Political Science, Sociology, Psychology |
| Computer Science | Mathematics, Computer Science                     |

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners should independently verify the eligibility requirements of
  universities, colleges, professional courses, and competitive examinations
  before selecting subjects.
</Callout>

## Practical Subjects

Some subjects contain practical components.

For these subjects:

- Practical examinations are compulsory.
- Practical files or records may be required.
- Formative Assessment requirements may apply.
- Practical marks are awarded separately.

## Subject Changes After Admission

NIOS may permit subject changes under prescribed rules and timelines.

However:

- Subject changes may be subject to fees.
- Deadlines may apply.
- Certain restrictions may exist.
- Not all requests may be approved.

<Callout type="warning">
  Carefully choose your subjects during admission. Changing subjects later may
  involve additional procedures, fees, and restrictions.
</Callout>
