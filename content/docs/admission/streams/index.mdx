# Admission Streams [toc]

NIOS offers different admission streams to accommodate learners with varying educational backgrounds and objectives. Choosing the correct stream is one of the most important steps during admission, as eligibility requirements, documentation requirements, and examination options may differ between streams.

This section explains the purpose of each stream and helps learners identify the most appropriate category before applying.

## Quick Comparison

| Stream   | Purpose                                   | Examination Mode      |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Stream 1 | Fresh Admission                           | Public Examination    |
| Stream 2 | Additional / Transfer / Failed Candidates | Public Examination    |
| Stream 3 | Secondary ODE Admission                   | On-Demand Examination |
| Stream 4 | Senior Secondary ODE Admission            | On-Demand Examination |

## Which Stream Should I Choose?

### Choose Stream 1 If

- You are applying to NIOS for the first time.
- You want to complete Secondary (Class 10) or Senior Secondary (Class 12).
- You are seeking admission through the regular NIOS public examination system.

### Choose Stream 2 If

- You have previously appeared in examinations conducted by a recognized board.
- You wish to avail Transfer of Credit (TOC) for eligible subjects.
- You have failed a recognized board examination and wish to complete your certification through NIOS.
- You wish to improve your previous academic qualifications.
- You satisfy the Stream 2 eligibility requirements prescribed by NIOS.

### Choose Stream 3 If

- You wish to appear through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.
- You are applying for Secondary (Class 10) level certification.

### Choose Stream 4 If

- You wish to appear through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.
- You are applying for Senior Secondary (Class 12) level certification.

## Understanding Block 1 and Block 2

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 1 admissions are conducted through two admission blocks commonly
  referred to as <strong>Block 1</strong> and <strong>Block 2</strong>. The
  block in which you take admission determines your first eligible examination
  session.
</Callout>

Detailed admission schedules, eligibility periods, and examination sessions are
explained on the Stream 1 page.

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Selecting the wrong stream may lead to admission complications, document
  issues, or ineligibility for the intended examination scheme. Always review
  the eligibility criteria before submitting your application.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  If you are unsure which stream applies to you, start by reviewing the Stream 1
  page, as it is the most commonly used admission category for new learners.
</Callout>
