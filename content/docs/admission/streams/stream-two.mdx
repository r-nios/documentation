# Stream 2 [toc]

Stream 2 is intended for learners who have previously appeared in examinations conducted by a recognized board and wish to complete, improve, or continue their education through NIOS.

This stream is commonly used by learners who:

- Have failed a recognized board examination.
- Wish to avail Transfer of Credit (TOC).
- Want to improve their previous academic performance.
- Need certification through an alternative pathway.

## What is Stream 2?

Unlike Stream 1, which is primarily intended for fresh admissions, Stream 2 is designed for learners who already have a previous academic record from a recognized board.

NIOS allows eligible learners to use their previous academic performance in accordance with applicable rules and eligibility conditions.

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 2 is not intended for first-time learners who have never appeared in a
  recognized board examination. Such learners generally apply through Stream 1.
</Callout>

## Who Should Choose Stream 2?

Choose Stream 2 if:

- You have previously appeared in examinations conducted by a recognized board.
- You have failed a recognized board examination and wish to complete certification through NIOS.
- You wish to avail Transfer of Credit (TOC) for eligible subjects.
- You want to improve your academic qualifications.
- You satisfy the eligibility requirements prescribed by NIOS.

## Understanding Transfer of Credit (TOC)

Transfer of Credit (TOC) allows eligible learners to transfer marks from subjects previously passed through a recognized board.

### Important Points

- TOC is not automatic.
- Only eligible subjects may be transferred.
- NIOS rules and subject equivalence requirements apply.
- Documentation must be submitted for verification.
- Approval is subject to NIOS scrutiny.

- Practical passing requirements must be satisfied separately where applicable.

## Common Misconceptions

### "Stream 2 is only for failed students."

No. Stream 2 is also used by learners seeking Transfer of Credit (TOC) and other eligible academic pathways.

### "All previous marks are automatically transferred."

No. Transfer of Credit is governed by NIOS rules and eligibility conditions.

### "Stream 2 learners cannot choose new subjects."

Subject selection remains subject to NIOS regulations and course requirements.

### "Stream 2 is easier than Stream 1."

Both streams ultimately require learners to satisfy NIOS academic and examination requirements.
