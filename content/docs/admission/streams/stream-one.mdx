# Stream 1 (Block 1 & Block 2) [toc]

Stream 1 is the most common admission category in NIOS and is intended for learners seeking fresh admission to the Secondary (Class 10) or Senior Secondary (Class 12) course.

Most first-time NIOS learners, school dropouts, private candidates, working professionals, athletes, artists, and other learners seeking flexible education are admitted through Stream 1.

## What is Stream 1?

Stream 1 is the standard NIOS admission stream for learners who wish to pursue Secondary or Senior Secondary education through the Open and Distance Learning (ODL) system.

Under this stream, learners:

- Study using Self Learning Materials (SLMs).
- Appear in NIOS Public Examinations.
- Assigned a Study Centre (AI).
- Can participate in PCP sessions where applicable.
- Must complete TMA requirements (where applicable).
- Must appear for practical examinations if enrolled in practical subjects.

## Blocks Meaning

Stream 1 admissions are conducted through two admission cycles known as Block 1 and Block 2.

### Block 1

Generally intended for learners wishing to appear in the next April/May Public Examination session.

### Block 2

Generally intended for learners wishing to appear in the next October/November Public Examination session.

<Callout type="info">
  Admission dates and examination eligibility periods may be revised by NIOS.
  Always refer to the latest admission notification before applying.
</Callout>

## Who Should Choose Stream 1?

Choose Stream 1 if:

- You are applying to NIOS for the first time.
- You wish to complete Secondary (Class 10).
- You wish to complete Senior Secondary (Class 12) after passing Secondary (Class 10).
- You are not applying under On-Demand Examination (ODE).
- You do not require admission through Stream 2 provisions.
- You want to appear in regular NIOS Public Examinations.

## Study Centres (AIs)

Stream 1 learners are assigned a Study Centre (Accredited Institution).

Study Centres may:

- Conduct PCP sessions.
- Conduct practical examinations.
- Provide learner support.
- Share important updates.
- Assist with practical-related activities.

<Callout type="tip">
  It is a good idea to establish contact with your Study Centre soon after your
  admission is confirmed.
</Callout>

## Tutor Marked Assignments (TMAs)

Tutor Marked Assignments (TMAs) form an important part of the NIOS assessment system.

TMAs:

- Are submitted online.
- Contribute towards assessment as prescribed by NIOS.
- Must be completed before the applicable deadlines.
- Are generally applicable to Stream 1 learners.

For detailed information regarding TMA submission, evaluation, marks, and deadlines, please refer to the dedicated TMA section.

## Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs)

NIOS may conduct Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs) through Study Centres.

PCPs are intended for:

- Doubt clarification.
- Academic guidance.
- Practical support.
- Learner interaction.

They are not intended to replace self-study or regular classroom teaching.

## Practical Subjects

If you select practical subjects:

- Practical examinations are compulsory.
- Practical records/files may be required.
- Formative Assessment requirements may apply.
- Practical marks are awarded separately from theory marks.

<Callout type="warning">
  Passing a practical subject generally requires satisfying both theory and
  practical requirements as prescribed by NIOS.
</Callout>

## Examinations

Stream 1 learners generally appear in:

- Public Examinations.
- Practical Examinations (where applicable).

Examination schedules are announced by NIOS through official notifications and
datesheets.

## Common Misconceptions

### "NIOS is a regular school."

No. NIOS follows an Open and Distance Learning model where self-study is the
primary mode of learning.

### "Attendance is compulsory like a regular school."

Regular school attendance is not required. However, attendance requirements may
apply to practical activities and specific assessment components.

### "Study materials are only available in printed form."

NIOS provides printed books, PDFs, online materials, syllabus documents, sample
papers, and various digital resources.

### "I need a coaching institute to complete NIOS."

No. NIOS is designed for self-learning, and official study materials are
sufficient for most learners.
