# Stream 3 [toc]

Stream 3 is the NIOS admission stream for learners seeking **Secondary (Class 10) certification through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system**.

Unlike the regular Public Examination system, ODE allows eligible learners to appear for examinations when seats are available at designated ODE examination centres, providing greater flexibility in scheduling examinations.

## What is Stream 3?

Stream 3 is specifically designed for learners who wish to obtain a Secondary (Class 10) certificate through the NIOS On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.

The ODE system allows learners to:

- Register for ODE admission.
- Prepare at their own pace.
- Book examinations subject to seat availability.
- Appear for subjects individually.
- Improve performance through additional attempts as permitted by NIOS rules.

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 3 is only applicable to the Secondary (Class 10) course under the
  On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.
</Callout>

## Who Should Choose Stream 3?

Choose Stream 3 if:

- You wish to pursue Secondary (Class 10) certification through ODE.
- You prefer flexible examination scheduling.
- You want to appear for examinations without waiting for the next Public Examination session.
- You satisfy the eligibility conditions prescribed by NIOS.

## How ODE Differs from Public Examinations

| Feature              | Public Examination                  | ODE                              |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Examination Schedule | Fixed Dates                         | Flexible                         |
| Admission Stream     | Stream 1 / Stream 2                 | Stream 3                         |
| Examination Booking  | Automatic through exam registration | Subject to ODE slot availability |
| Examination Attempts | Public Examination Sessions         | ODE Scheduling System            |

## Study Centres and PCPs

<Callout type="warning">
  Stream 3 learners generally do not receive a Study Centre (AI) allocation.
</Callout>

As a result:

- No regular PCP allocation is provided.
- No Study Centre support is generally assigned.
- Learners are primarily responsible for self-study and examination preparation.

This is one of the major differences between Stream 1 and Stream 3 admissions.

## Practical Subjects

If you choose practical subjects under Stream 3:

- Practical requirements continue to apply.
- Practical examinations remain compulsory where prescribed.
- Practical passing requirements must be satisfied separately.
- ODE learners may be required to follow special practical examination procedures prescribed by NIOS.

## Advantages of Stream 3

- Flexible examination scheduling.
- Faster examination opportunities.
- Self-paced preparation.
- Reduced dependence on fixed examination cycles.
- Suitable for learners requiring flexibility.

## Common Misconceptions

### "ODE means easier examinations."

No. The syllabus, academic standards, and certification requirements remain governed by NIOS.

### "ODE learners do not need practical examinations."

Incorrect. Practical requirements continue to apply wherever prescribed.

### "Stream 3 learners receive Study Centres."

Generally, Study Centres are not allotted to Stream 3 learners.

### "ODE certificates are different."

NIOS certificates issued through ODE carry the same recognition as other NIOS certifications.
