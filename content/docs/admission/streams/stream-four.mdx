# Stream 4 [toc]

Stream 4 is the NIOS admission stream for learners seeking **Senior Secondary (Class 12) certification through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system**.

It provides a flexible alternative to the regular Public Examination system by allowing eligible learners to appear for examinations when they feel prepared, subject to seat availability and NIOS guidelines.

## What is Stream 4?

Stream 4 is specifically designed for learners who wish to pursue **Senior Secondary (Class 12)** certification through the NIOS On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.

The ODE system enables learners to:

- Study at their own pace.
- Schedule examinations based on readiness.
- Appear for subjects individually.
- Benefit from greater flexibility than regular Public Examinations.
- Complete certification without waiting for fixed examination cycles.

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 4 is only applicable to the Senior Secondary (Class 12) course under
  the NIOS On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.
</Callout>

## Who Should Choose Stream 4?

Choose Stream 4 if:

- You wish to obtain Senior Secondary (Class 12) certification through ODE.
- You require flexible examination scheduling.
- You do not wish to wait for the next Public Examination session.
- You are comfortable with self-paced preparation.
- You satisfy the eligibility requirements prescribed by NIOS.

## ODE vs Public Examinations

| Feature              | Public Examination               | ODE                         |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Examination Schedule | Fixed Dates                      | Flexible                    |
| Admission Stream     | Stream 1 / Stream 2              | Stream 4                    |
| Examination Booking  | Through examination registration | Through ODE scheduling      |
| Examination Attempts | Fixed examination sessions       | Subject to ODE availability |

## Study Centres and PCPs

<Callout type="warning">
  Stream 4 learners generally do not receive a Study Centre (AI) allocation.
</Callout>

As a result:

- Regular PCP allocation is generally not available.
- No Study Centre support is usually assigned.
- Learners are expected to prepare independently using NIOS resources.

This is one of the major differences between Stream 1 and Stream 4 admissions.

## Practical Subjects

If you choose practical subjects under Stream 4:

- Practical examinations remain compulsory.
- Practical files or records may be required.
- Practical passing requirements continue to apply.
- ODE-specific practical procedures may be prescribed by NIOS.

## Advantages of Stream 4

- Flexible examination scheduling.
- Self-paced preparation.
- Faster opportunities to appear for examinations.
- Suitable for working professionals and independent learners.
- Reduced dependence on fixed examination cycles.

## Common Misconceptions

### "ODE is easier than Public Examinations."

No. The syllabus, certification standards, and academic requirements remain governed by NIOS.

### "Practical examinations are not required."

Incorrect. Practical requirements continue to apply wherever prescribed.

### "Stream 4 learners receive Study Centres."

Generally, Study Centres are not allotted to Stream 4 learners.

### "ODE certificates are different from regular NIOS certificates."

No. NIOS certificates issued through ODE have the same recognition as other NIOS certificates.

### "I can appear whenever I want."

Examinations are subject to seat availability, subject availability, and NIOS operational guidelines.
