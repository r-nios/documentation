# Eligibility [toc]

Before applying for admission, learners should verify that they meet the eligibility requirements prescribed by NIOS. Eligibility requirements may vary depending on the course level and admission stream.

This page provides a general overview of NIOS eligibility requirements. Detailed eligibility conditions applicable to specific streams can be found on their respective pages.

## Eligibility by Course

### Secondary (Class 10)

To be eligible for admission to the Secondary course, learners must satisfy the minimum age requirement prescribed by NIOS.

| Requirement            | Details                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Minimum Age            | 14 years as on 31 July (Block 1) or 31 January (Block 2) |
| Previous Qualification | No formal educational qualification required             |

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS does not require a Class 8 or Class 9 pass certificate for admission to
  the Secondary course. However, learners must satisfy the minimum age
  requirement.
</Callout>

### Senior Secondary (Class 12)

To be eligible for admission to the Senior Secondary course, learners must have successfully passed Secondary (Class 10) or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.

| Requirement           | Details                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Minimum Qualification | Passed Secondary (Class 10) or equivalent |
| Supporting Document   | Class 10 Marksheet / Certificate          |

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners applying for Senior Secondary admission must provide valid proof of
  having passed the Secondary examination from a recognized board.
</Callout>

## Stream-wise Overview

| Stream   | General Eligibility                                                                      |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stream 1 | Fresh admission learners satisfying course eligibility requirements                      |
| Stream 2 | Learners with previous board examination records and eligible TOC/failed candidate cases |
| Stream 3 | Learners eligible for Secondary admission under the ODE system                           |
| Stream 4 | Learners eligible for Senior Secondary admission under the ODE system                    |

## Age Requirements

Age eligibility is determined according to NIOS rules applicable to the chosen admission block.

| Course           | Minimum Age                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Secondary        | 14 Years                              |
| Senior Secondary | Secondary pass qualification required |

<Callout type="tip">
  Ensure that the date of birth entered during admission matches the supporting
  documents submitted for verification.
</Callout>

## Qualification Requirements

### Secondary Course

- No prior academic qualification is mandatory.
- Minimum age requirements must be satisfied.

### Senior Secondary Course

- Secondary (Class 10) pass certificate from a recognized board is required.
- Supporting educational documents must be submitted during admission.

## Special Cases

### Transfer of Credit (TOC)

Learners applying under Stream 2 and seeking Transfer of Credit (TOC) must satisfy the eligibility requirements prescribed by NIOS for TOC.

### Failed Candidates

Learners who have appeared in examinations conducted by recognized boards and have not successfully completed certification may be eligible to apply through Stream 2, subject to NIOS rules.

## Important Notes

<Callout type="warning">
  Meeting the basic eligibility requirements does not automatically guarantee
  admission approval. All submitted information and documents are subject to
  verification by NIOS.
</Callout>

  Eligibility requirements, admission rules, and documentation requirements may
  change from time to time. Always refer to the latest NIOS admission
  notification for official requirements.
</Callout>
