# Required Documents [toc]

Before starting the admission process, learners should ensure that all required documents are available and ready for upload. Submitting clear and accurate documents helps prevent admission delays, verification issues, and correction requests.

The exact documents required may vary depending on the course, admission stream, category claimed, and individual circumstances.

## Identity Documents

Acceptable identity documents generally include government-issued documents containing the learner's name and identifying information.

Examples may include:

- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Voter ID Card
- Driving Licence
- Other government-issued identity documents accepted by NIOS

<Callout type="info">
  The name entered during admission should exactly match the name appearing on
  the supporting identity documents.
</Callout>

## Date of Birth Proof

NIOS may require proof of date of birth, particularly for Secondary admissions where minimum age criteria apply.

Commonly accepted documents may include:

- Birth Certificate issued by a competent authority
- Aadhaar Card (where accepted)
- School Leaving Certificate
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Previous School Record
- Other age-proof documents accepted by NIOS

<Callout type="warning">
  The date of birth entered during admission should exactly match the date of
  birth shown on the supporting document.
</Callout>

## Address Proof

Where address verification is required, learners may need to provide a valid address document.

Examples may include:

- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility Bills (where accepted)
- Government-issued address documents
- Other documents accepted by NIOS

## Category & Reservation Documents

Learners claiming benefits under reserved or special categories may be required to submit supporting documents.

Examples include:

- Scheduled Caste (SC) Certificate
- Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate
- Other Backward Class (OBC) Certificate
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Certificate
- Disability Certificate (PwD), where applicable

<Callout type="info">
  Reservation or category-related benefits can only be claimed if the relevant
  supporting documents are submitted and accepted during the admission process.
</Callout>

## Educational Documents

### Secondary (Class 10)

For Secondary admission, learners primarily need to satisfy the minimum age requirement prescribed by NIOS.

Depending on the admission category, supporting educational documents may also be required.

### Senior Secondary (Class 12)

For Senior Secondary admission, learners are generally required to submit:

- Secondary (Class 10) Marksheet
- Secondary (Class 10) Pass Certificate
- Equivalent Qualification Certificate, where applicable

<Callout type="warning">
  Senior Secondary learners must provide proof of having successfully completed
  the Secondary examination from a recognized board.
</Callout>

## Stream-Specific Documents

Certain admission streams may require additional documentation.

### Stream 1

Typically requires:

- Photograph
- Identity Proof
- Date of Birth Proof
- Address Proof
- Educational Documents (where applicable)

### Stream 2

May additionally require:

- Previous Board Marksheet
- Previous Board Certificate
- Transfer of Credit (TOC) related documents
- Failed examination records, where applicable

### Stream 3

May require documents prescribed for Secondary admission under the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.

### Stream 4

May require documents prescribed for Senior Secondary admission under the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.

## Photograph Guidelines

Your photograph should generally:

- Be recent.
- Clearly show your face.
- Have adequate lighting.
- Be free from excessive editing or filters.
- Follow the specifications prescribed by NIOS.

## Signature Upload

Where required:

- Upload a clear image of your signature.
- Use a plain background.
- Ensure the signature is clearly visible.
- Avoid blurry or low-resolution images.

## Document Upload Guidelines

When uploading documents:

- Use clear and readable scans.
- Ensure all information is visible.
- Avoid cropped images.
- Verify that the correct document is uploaded in the correct category.
- Use the prescribed file format and size requirements.
- Review uploads before final submission.

<Callout type="tip">
  If possible, use scanned copies instead of mobile photographs to improve
  readability and reduce verification issues.
</Callout>

## If a Document is Missing

If you do not possess a required document:

1. Check whether an alternative document is accepted by NIOS.
2. Obtain a duplicate copy from the issuing authority if necessary.
3. Review current NIOS admission guidelines.
4. Contact NIOS support if clarification is required.

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not upload incorrect, forged, or unrelated documents merely to complete the
  application process. This may result in rejection, cancellation, or additional
  verification requirements.
</Callout>
