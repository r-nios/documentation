# Admission Overview [toc]

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) offers a flexible admission system that allows learners from diverse educational backgrounds to continue or complete their education through Open and Distance Learning (ODL).

Whether you are a school student, working professional, dropout, athlete, artist, or someone seeking a second opportunity in education, NIOS provides multiple admission streams designed to meet different learner needs.

## Who Can Apply?

NIOS admissions are open to a wide range of learners, including:

- Students seeking Secondary (Class 10) certification.
- Students seeking Senior Secondary (Class 12) certification.
- School dropouts wishing to continue their education.
- Learners pursuing education alongside employment or training.
- Sportspersons and performing artists requiring flexible study schedules.
- Learners appearing through the On-Demand Examination (ODE) system.
