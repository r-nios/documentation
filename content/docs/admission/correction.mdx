# Admission Corrections [toc]

During admission verification, NIOS may identify discrepancies in the information or documents submitted by a learner. In such cases, the application may be returned for correction before admission approval can be granted.

Unlike some examination processes, NIOS generally does not open a separate public correction window for admission applications. Corrections are usually requested individually when discrepancies are found during verification.

## How Corrections Work

After admission submission and fee payment:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Application Verification**

    The concerned Regional Centre reviews the application, documents, and
    admission details.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Discrepancy Identified**

    If any issue is found, such as missing documents, mismatched information, or
    incorrect uploads, the application may be marked for correction.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Notification Issued**

    A correction notification is generally issued through the SDMIS portal.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Learner Submits Corrections**

    The learner reviews the remarks and submits the required corrections.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Re-Verification**

    The corrected application is reviewed again by the Regional Centre.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Further Processing**

    Once verified, the application is forwarded for further approval.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Are Correction Requests Sent?

Correction requests are generally communicated through the NIOS SDMIS portal.

Learners should regularly monitor:

- SDMIS Dashboard
- Admission Status Portal
- Registered Email Address (where applicable)
- Notifications displayed in the learner account

<Callout type="warning">
  NIOS generally does not provide a separate admission correction window for all
  learners. Correction requests are usually issued individually when
  discrepancies are identified during verification.
</Callout>

## What Should You Do If a Correction Is Requested?

If a correction request is received:

1. Carefully read the remarks provided by NIOS.
2. Identify the specific issue requiring correction.
3. Upload the required document or corrected information.
4. Verify all details before resubmitting.
5. Monitor the portal for further updates.

<Callout type="tip">
  Respond to correction requests as soon as possible to avoid delays in
  admission verification and approval.
</Callout>

## After Corrections Are Submitted

Once corrections are submitted:

- The application returns to the verification process.
- Documents are reviewed again.
- Additional clarification may be requested if necessary.
- The application may then be approved and forwarded for further processing.
