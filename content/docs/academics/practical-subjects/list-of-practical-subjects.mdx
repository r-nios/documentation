# List of Practical Subjects [toc]

This page lists NIOS subjects that include practical components. For practical subjects, the practical marks are divided equally between:

- **Formative Assessment (FA)** — 50% of practical marks
- **Practical Examination** — 50% of practical marks

For example, if a subject has 20 practical marks, 10 marks are allotted to FA and 10 marks are allotted to the practical examination.

## Passing Criteria for Practical Subjects

Learners must satisfy the minimum passing requirements prescribed by NIOS for both theory and practical components.

### Secondary (Class 10)

To pass a practical subject at the Secondary level, a learner must:

- Obtain at least **33% marks in the theory examination**.
- Obtain at least **33% marks in the practical component**.
- Meet the overall passing requirements prescribed by NIOS.

### Senior Secondary (Class 12)

To pass a practical subject at the Senior Secondary level, a learner must:

- Obtain at least **33% marks separately in Theory and Practical**.
- Obtain the required aggregate marks as prescribed by NIOS.

<Callout type="warning">
  Practical components are evaluated separately. A learner who passes the theory
  examination but fails the practical component will not be considered to have
  passed the subject until the practical requirement is fulfilled.
</Callout>

## Improvement and Reappearance in Practical Subjects

NIOS provides different provisions for Secondary and Senior Secondary learners who fail in the practical component.

### Secondary (Class 10)

If a learner fails the practical component of a subject at the Secondary level, they are generally required to:

- Reappear in the subject again.
- Appear for both the theory and practical examinations as applicable.

Practical marks are not carried forward separately for certification purposes at the Secondary level.

### Senior Secondary (Class 12)

At the Senior Secondary level, learners who have passed the theory examination but failed the practical component may be allowed to:

- Reappear only for the practical component.
- Pay the prescribed examination fee for the practical examination.
- Retain their theory performance as per applicable NIOS rules.

This enables learners to improve or clear the practical requirement without reappearing for the theory examination.

<Callout type="info">
  Senior Secondary learners should verify the latest NIOS examination
  notification and fee schedule before applying for a practical re-examination,
  as procedures and fees may be revised from time to time.
</Callout>

## How Practical Marks Are Calculated

The practical marks shown in the tables above are divided equally between Formative Assessment (FA) and the Practical Examination (SA).

Examples:

| Practical Marks |  FA |  SA |
| --------------- | --: | --: |
| 15              | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| 20              |  10 |  10 |
| 30              |  15 |  15 |
| 40              |  20 |  20 |
| 60              |  30 |  30 |
| 70              |  35 |  35 |

The final practical score is calculated by combining the marks obtained in both components.

## Understanding the Assessment Structure

| Component             | Description                                                     |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Theory                | Written examination conducted by NIOS.                          |
| Practical             | Total practical marks allotted to the subject.                  |
| FA                    | Formative Assessment component (50% of practical marks).        |
| Practical Examination | Final practical examination component (50% of practical marks). |
| Total                 | Maximum marks for the subject.                                  |

<Callout type="info">
  The FA and Practical Examination components together constitute the total
  practical marks allotted to the subject.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners must appear in both the theory and practical examination components
  wherever applicable. Failure to appear in the practical examination may affect
  certification.
</Callout>
