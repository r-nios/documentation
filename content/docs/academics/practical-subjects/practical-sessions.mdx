# Practical Sessions [toc]

Practical sessions are an important part of NIOS education for subjects that include practical, laboratory, studio, performance, or skill-based components. These sessions provide learners with hands-on experience and help develop the skills required for practical examinations.

Practical sessions are generally conducted through the learner's designated Accredited Institution (AI) and may be compulsory for certain subjects.

## Why Practical Sessions Matter

Practical sessions help learners:

- Understand concepts through hands-on learning.
- Develop subject-specific practical skills.
- Complete laboratory work and practical activities.
- Prepare for practical examinations.
- Complete projects, records, and assignments.
- Improve performance in Formative Assessment (FA) and Practical Examinations.

## Who Needs to Attend?

Learners enrolled in subjects with practical components should attend practical sessions organized by their Accredited Institution.

Practical sessions are particularly important for subjects such as:

- Science & Technology
- Home Science
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Geography
- Computer Science
- Data Entry Operations
- Painting
- Physical Education
- Music and Performing Arts subjects
- Other vocational and practical subjects

<Callout type="warning">
  Attendance in practical sessions may be required to complete practical work,
  projects, records, and assessments. Learners should follow the instructions
  issued by their Accredited Institution.
</Callout>

## Scheduling of Practical Sessions

Practical sessions are usually scheduled by the Accredited Institution (AI) assigned to the learner.

The schedule may vary depending on:

- Subject requirements.
- Availability of laboratories and facilities.
- Number of enrolled learners.
- Examination schedules.
- Institutional arrangements.

Learners should regularly stay in contact with their Accredited Institution for updates regarding practical classes and practical examination schedules.

## What to Bring to Practical Sessions

Depending on the subject, learners may be required to bring:

- Practical records and notebooks.
- Assignment files.
- Drawing and art materials.
- Laboratory manuals.
- Project reports.
- Identification documents.
- Other subject-specific materials.

## Preparation Tips

To make the most of practical sessions:

1. Attend all scheduled practical classes.
2. Complete practical records on time.
3. Practice experiments and activities regularly.
4. Follow instructions provided by teachers and instructors.
5. Maintain project files and supporting documents carefully.
6. Prepare for viva voce and practical examinations.

## Practical Examinations

Practical sessions help learners prepare for practical examinations conducted by NIOS.

Practical examinations may include:

- Performance-based assessment.
- Experiments and demonstrations.
- Viva voce.
- Practical records and project evaluation.
- Skill demonstrations.

<Callout type="info">
  For practical subjects, the practical component generally consists of
  Formative Assessment (FA) and a Practical Examination. Learners should refer
  to the practical subject guidelines and examination scheme applicable to their
  course.
</Callout>
