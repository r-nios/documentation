## Flexibility in Subject Selection

One of the key advantages of NIOS is the flexibility it offers in choosing subjects. Unlike many conventional school systems, learners can select a combination of subjects that aligns with their academic goals, career aspirations, and personal interests.

This flexible approach allows learners to:

- Combine subjects from different disciplines.
- Choose subjects based on future higher education requirements.
- Pursue academic and vocational learning pathways.
- Continue education without being restricted by rigid stream structures.
- Customize their learning experience according to their individual needs.

The flexibility offered by NIOS makes it particularly beneficial for working professionals, sportspersons, artists, learners with special needs, and those seeking alternative pathways to education.

## Alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP)

The flexible academic structure of NIOS aligns closely with the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which encourages multidisciplinary learning, learner choice, and greater academic mobility.

NIOS supports several key NEP principles, including:

- Flexibility in subject selection.
- Promotion of multidisciplinary education.
- Integration of vocational and skill-based learning.
- Learner-centric and self-paced education.
- Multiple pathways for lifelong learning.

By offering diverse subject choices and flexible learning opportunities, NIOS helps learners build educational pathways that match their individual aspirations and future goals.
