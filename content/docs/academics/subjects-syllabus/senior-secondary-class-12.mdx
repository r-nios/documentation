# Senior Secondary (Class 12) [toc]

The NIOS Senior Secondary Course (Class 12) provides learners with a flexible and multidisciplinary curriculum that allows them to select subjects according to their academic interests, higher education goals, and career aspirations. Unlike traditional stream-based systems, NIOS enables learners to combine subjects from different disciplines while maintaining a structured examination and certification framework.

Learners are generally required to choose a minimum of five subjects for certification, including at least one language subject.

## Subject Selection Rules

For certification, learners should normally:

- Select a minimum of five subjects.
- Choose at least one language subject from Group A.
- Select the remaining subjects from Groups B, C, D, E, and F.
- Choose only one subject from each of Groups C, D, E, and F.
- Additional subjects may be taken as per NIOS rules and prescribed fees.

<Callout type="info">
  Five subjects may be chosen with either one or two languages from Group A and
  the remaining subjects from Groups B, C, D, E, and F (only one subject from
  each of Groups C, D, E, and F).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Two additional subjects may be taken from any group (only one subject each
  from Groups C, D, E, and F) with additional fees as per NIOS norms.
</Callout>

## Flexibility and Multidisciplinary Learning

One of the major advantages of NIOS is its flexible academic structure. Learners are not restricted to conventional Science, Commerce, or Humanities streams and may create subject combinations that align with their individual goals.

Examples include:

- Physics with Political Science and Computer Science.
- Biology with Psychology and Sociology.
- Accountancy with Economics and Mathematics.
- History with Environmental Science and Tourism.

This multidisciplinary approach aligns with the objectives of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which encourages learner choice, flexibility, and lifelong learning.

## Vocational Integration with Academic Subjects

NIOS allows eligible learners to integrate vocational subjects with academic subjects.

### Vocational Courses Available in Combination with Academic Subjects

| Code | Course                              |
| ---- | ----------------------------------- |
| 631  | Computer & Office Applications      |
| 660  | Web Development                     |
| 663  | Preservation of Fruits & Vegetables |
| 667  | Yoga Assistant                      |

Under this scheme:

- One vocational subject may be taken along with academic subjects.
- The vocational subject is counted as one of the five certification subjects.
- Vocational subjects are offered through the same Accredited Institution (AI) selected for academic studies.
- Assessment follows the prescribed NIOS examination and practical scheme.

### Important Information

- Internal assessment may form part of the evaluation process.
- Vocational subjects follow the same passing criteria as academic subjects.
- On-Demand Examinations (ODEs) are currently not available for vocational subjects integrated with academic courses.
- Vocational course materials are generally available in English and Hindi medium.
