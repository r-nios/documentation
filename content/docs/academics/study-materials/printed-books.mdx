# Printed Books [toc]

NIOS provides official **Self Learning Materials (SLMs)** for all academic subjects offered under the Secondary and Senior Secondary courses. These materials are specially designed for self-paced learning and serve as the primary resource for examination preparation.

The books are available online through the official NIOS Course Material Portal and can be accessed subject-wise.

## About NIOS Study Materials

NIOS study materials are developed specifically for open and distance learning. Each subject is presented in a learner-friendly format and generally includes:

- Learning objectives
- Detailed explanations and examples
- Activities and exercises
- Self-check questions
- Chapter summaries
- Examination-oriented content

These materials are aligned with the latest NIOS curriculum and syllabus.
