# Personal Contact Programmes (PCP) [toc]

NIOS follows an open and distance learning system where learners primarily study independently using Self Learning Materials (SLMs). To support learners during their studies, NIOS conducts **Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs)** through Accredited Institutions (AIs), commonly known as Study Centres.

PCPs provide learners with an opportunity to interact with teachers, clarify doubts, receive academic guidance, and participate in practical activities where applicable.

## What is a PCP?

A Personal Contact Programme (PCP) is a learner support mechanism designed to assist students who may require guidance while studying independently.

The purpose of PCPs is to:

- Clarify academic doubts.
- Provide subject-related guidance.
- Assist learners in understanding difficult concepts.
- Support practical learning activities.
- Facilitate Formative Assessment where applicable.

PCPs are **not intended to replace self-study** and should not be viewed as regular classroom teaching.

## Theory PCPs

For theory subjects, NIOS provides:

- **30 PCP sessions** in total.
- **15 sessions are compulsory**.
- Conducted through the learner's assigned Study Centre (AI).

These sessions focus on doubt clarification, guidance, and academic support.

## Practical PCPs

Subjects that contain practical components receive additional practical PCP sessions.

NIOS generally provides:

- **5 Practical PCP sessions** for learning activities and Formative Assessment.
- **1 Final Practical Session** for Summative Assessment.

The practical PCPs are conducted face-to-face at the assigned Study Centre.

## Practical Assessment Structure

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Attend Practical PCP Sessions**

    Learners participate in practical learning activities conducted by the
    Study Centre.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Formative Assessment (FA)**

    The first five practical PCP sessions contribute towards the Formative
    Assessment component.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Summative Assessment**

    The final practical session is used for the practical examination
    (Summative Assessment).

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Final Practical Marks**

    Practical marks are generally divided equally between Formative Assessment
    and the final practical examination.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Clarification About FA and PCP

<Callout type="warning">
  Many learners use terms such as "FA PCP", "5 PCP", or "PCP Marks" when
  referring to the Formative Assessment component of practical subjects.

While Formative Assessment activities are conducted during practical PCP
sessions, **PCP and Formative Assessment are not the same thing**.

PCP refers to the contact sessions conducted by the Study Centre, whereas
Formative Assessment is an assessment component that may be carried out during
those sessions.

For detailed information regarding Formative Assessment, assessment criteria,
marks distribution, and submission requirements, please refer to the
dedicated **Formative Assessment (FA)** section of this documentation.

</Callout>

## Attendance Requirements

<Callout type="info">
  Attendance in practical PCP sessions is compulsory for learners enrolled in
  subjects containing practical components.
</Callout>

It is strongly recommended that learners attend all available PCP sessions to
maximize academic support and examination preparedness.

## When Are PCPs Conducted?

Practical PCP sessions are generally conducted:

| Admission Block | Typical PCP Period    |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| Block I         | From November onwards |
| Block II        | From May onwards      |

Actual schedules may vary depending on the Study Centre and NIOS notifications.

## What PCPs Are Not

<Callout type="warning">
  PCPs are not intended to cover the entire syllabus through classroom teaching.

Learners should not expect full-time classroom instruction similar to that
provided in conventional schools. Self-study remains the primary mode of
learning in the NIOS system.

</Callout>

The role of PCP faculty is to:

- Clarify doubts.
- Guide learners.
- Assist with practical activities.
- Provide academic support where required.

## Fees and Charges

<Callout type="tip">
  Learners should not pay any additional amount to their Accredited Institution
  (AI) or Study Centre for attending PCP sessions.
</Callout>

PCP-related charges are already covered under the applicable NIOS fee structure.
