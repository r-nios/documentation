# Overview [toc]

The Academics section covers everything related to learning under the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), including subjects, syllabus, study materials, practical examinations, academic support, and learner assistance services.

Whether you are a newly admitted learner or preparing for examinations, this section provides the information and resources needed throughout your academic journey.

## What You'll Learn

By exploring this section, you'll understand:

- Available subjects and syllabus structure
- Practical and theory-based subjects
- Official study materials and learning resources
- Personal Contact Programmes (PCP)
- Study centres and academic support services
- Important academic guidelines

## Academic Journey

Learners typically engage with the academic system through the following stages:

1. Select subjects during admission.
2. Study using official NIOS learning materials.
3. Attend PCP classes when available.
4. Complete practical work for applicable subjects.
5. Appear in theory and practical examinations.
6. Receive academic support through study centres and learner services.

### Important Notes

<Callout type="info">
  NIOS follows a flexible learning model that allows learners to study at their
  own pace while accessing academic support through study centres and online
  resources.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners should always refer to the latest official notifications for syllabus
  updates, practical requirements, and examination-related academic
  instructions.
</Callout>
