# Study Centres & Academic Support [toc]

NIOS Accredited Institutions (AIs), commonly referred to as **Study Centres**, are local institutions that provide learner support services. Depending on your stream and subjects, your Study Centre may assist with Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs), practical examinations, Formative Assessment activities, and learner guidance.

## What is a Study Centre?

A Study Centre (AI) is an institution accredited by NIOS to provide academic and administrative support to learners.

## Role of Study Centres

Study Centres may:

- Conduct PCP (Personal Contact Programme) sessions.
- Conduct practical examinations.
- Conduct practical-related activities and Formative Assessment sessions where applicable.
- Share examination and practical schedules.
- Assist learners with academic queries.
- Create learner WhatsApp groups or communication channels.
- Provide guidance regarding practical files and records.

According to NIOS PCP guidelines, Study Centres are responsible for conducting PCP sessions, informing learners about schedules, and uploading PCP timetables as prescribed by NIOS.

## Stream-wise Availability

| Stream         | Study Centre Available? | Purpose                                             |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Stream 1       | ✅ Yes                  | PCP, practicals, learner support, academic guidance |
| Stream 2       | ✅ Yes                  | Practical examinations and support where applicable |
| Stream 3 (ODE) | ❌ No                   | No Study Centre assigned                            |
| Stream 4 (ODE) | ❌ No                   | No Study Centre assigned                            |

<Callout type="info">
  Stream 3 and Stream 4 learners generally do not receive a Study Centre
  allocation because they are enrolled through the On-Demand Examination (ODE)
  system.
</Callout>

## When Should You Contact Your Study Centre?

Many learners wait until practical examinations are announced. However, it is advisable to establish contact much earlier.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Complete Admission**

    Ensure that your admission has been successfully confirmed by NIOS.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Save Study Centre Details**

    Note the contact information available on your learner dashboard.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Introduce Yourself**

    Contact the Study Centre and inform them that you are a NIOS learner.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Ask Important Questions**

    Inquire about PCP sessions, practical schedules, communication groups, and
    any subject-specific requirements.

  </Step>

  <Step>
    **Stay Connected**

    Maintain communication with the Study Centre throughout your academic
    journey.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Questions to Ask

After your admission is confirmed, consider asking:

- Do you conduct PCP sessions?
- Is there a WhatsApp group for learners?
- How are practical schedules communicated?
- Do practical subjects require practical files?
- Who should be contacted for examination-related updates?
- When are PCP sessions usually conducted?

## PCP, FA PCP, and Practicals

<Callout type="warning">
  Learners often confuse PCP, FA PCP, Formative Assessment (FA), and practical
  examinations.

These are related concepts but they are not identical.

For detailed information regarding PCP sessions, practical PCPs, and
Formative Assessment, refer to the dedicated pages in this documentation.

</Callout>

In many practical subjects:

- PCP sessions may be conducted by the Study Centre.
- Practical guidance may be provided during these sessions.
- Practical files may be discussed and reviewed.
- Practical examinations are conducted offline at the Study Centre.

## How to Contact Your Study Centre

### Method 1 — Dashboard Contact Details

The easiest method is to use the Study Centre contact information available on
your learner dashboard.

### Method 2 — Search Online

If nobody answers the provided number:

1. Search the Study Centre online.
2. Try alternate contact numbers.
3. Request contact details of the NIOS Coordinator or NIOS In-charge.
4. Contact them through phone or WhatsApp.

### Method 3 — Visit Personally

If required, visit the Study Centre during working hours and speak directly
with the NIOS Coordinator.

## Practical Examinations

For learners with practical subjects:

- Practical examinations are conducted offline.
- Exact dates and timings are often communicated through the Study Centre.
- Practical files or records may be required depending on the subject.
- Attendance requirements may vary depending on the practical component.

<Callout type="tip">
  Do not wait until the last moment to inquire about practical examinations.
  Stay in touch with your Study Centre throughout the academic session.
</Callout>

## Scam Awareness

<Callout type="warning">
  Be cautious if anyone demands large amounts of money for services that are
  already covered under NIOS procedures.
</Callout>

### Common Red Flags

- Claims that admission is only possible through the Study Centre.
- Demands for excessive payments for practicals or PCP attendance.
- Claims that regular school attendance is mandatory for NIOS learners.
- Requests for unofficial fees without proper documentation.

### What You Normally Pay

Typically, learners only pay:

- Admission fees through NIOS.
- Examination fees through NIOS.
- Official fees notified by NIOS for specific services, if applicable.

Always verify fee-related information through official NIOS notifications.
